Water Delivery
V2 Scope Document | Preliminary | May 2026
Overview
Theme camps can apply for water delivery to site based on camp size. Currently managed through Quicket and
manual coordination — clunky and in need of integration into the main app.
User Stories
Theme Camp Lead
n I want to apply for water delivery based on my camp's registered size
n I want to see how much water my camp is allocated
n I want to pay for water through the app instead of Quicket
n I want to choose or understand the delivery schedule
n I want to request additional water beyond my allocation if possible
n I want to track my water deliveries during the event
AfricaBurn Staff
n I want to see total water demand across all camps
n I want to manage allocations based on camp size and availability
n I want to track payments for water across all camps
n I want to generate a delivery schedule for the water contractor
Business Rules & Constraints
n How is water allocation calculated? (litres per person per day?)
n What's the pricing model?
n Daily delivery or one-time bulk?
n Can camps exceed their allocation at extra cost?
n Is water delivery tied to registration?
Open Questions for AfricaBurn
n How much water is typically delivered per event?
