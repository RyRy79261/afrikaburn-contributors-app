AfricaBurn App
Master Project Brief | Preliminary Scope | May 2026
The Problem
AfricaBurn theme camps currently manage their obligations and logistics through a patchwork of Google Forms,
Quicket payments, WhatsApp messages, and manual coordination. This creates friction for everyone — theme
camp leads struggle with clunky, unresponsive forms; AfricaBurn staff spend time chasing information instead of
managing the event; and contractors lack clear, structured workflows for deliveries and logistics.
Last year, a standalone container transport app was built and went well — proving that a purpose-built tool can
improve at least one of these workflows. The opportunity now is to bring all theme camp logistics under one roof:
registration, container transport, water, ice, gas, and potentially more.
What this replaces:
(cid:127) Google Forms for theme camp registration
(cid:127) Quicket / manual systems for water delivery payments
(cid:127) Ad hoc coordination for ice and gas delivery
(cid:127) The previous standalone container transport app (fresh start)
User Families
There are three broad families of users. Within each family, there are specific roles with different permissions
and needs.
1. AfricaBurn Organisation Staff
The people who run AfricaBurn and need to manage, approve, and oversee everything. Specific staff roles are
still to be confirmed with AB.
2. Participants
People who attend AfricaBurn and manage a registered entity. Theme camps are the primary focus for V1.
Role Description Key Needs
Theme Camp Lead Primary contact / admin for the Registration, container booking, water/ice/gas
camp ordering, managing camp details, comms with AB
Alternative Contact Backup admin Same access as lead, acts as fallback
Container Collection Mandated to receive the Delivery time, sign-off, lightweight offline-capable
Person container on site experience
Mutant Vehicle Owners and Artwork Admins are noted as future user types (V3) but not scoped here.

Cross-Cutting Concerns
Things that apply across all workflows and will need decisions.
(cid:127) Authentication & identity — Users log in via email or OAuth (Google). Fairly standard auth setup.
(cid:127) Payments — Multiple workflows involve payment. Yoco is the likely payment provider. Refund handling,
payment plans TBD.
(cid:127) Notifications — Email confirmations for key actions (bookings, payments, schedule changes).

Priority Questions for AfricaBurn
These questions affect the fundamental shape of the app and should be answered early.
n Do artworks use the same logistics services as theme camps? (containers, water, ice, gas) — if yes, the
app serves ~220 registered projects, not just ~120 theme camps.
n Do artworks go through a similar registration form? — if comparable, we build one registration flow with
project-type variations rather than separate systems.
n Is the site map grid shared across theme camps and artworks? — both lists show code + location format. If
shared, placement is one unified process.
n Does placement happen inside this app or in a separate tool? — AB assigns camp codes and ERFs. If they
want to do that here, it's a significant feature.
n What about mutant vehicles? — do MVs also register and use logistics services?
Scale from 2026 data: ~120 theme camps, ~100 artworks. Both have codes and map locations assigned by
AB.
