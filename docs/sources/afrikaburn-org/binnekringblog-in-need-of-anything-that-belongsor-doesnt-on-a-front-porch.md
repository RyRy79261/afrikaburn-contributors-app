## Time Well Wasted is on a mission to build a piece of stillness amidst Burn’s playground in the form of a quaint front porch.

**We are looking for some donations like rocking chairs, a small table, chairs, a bench, a small bookshelf, suncatchers, wall decor, gnomes … anything you think could belong, or not, on a front porch.**

We hope you’ll consider contributing to something that will deepen the magical feeling of coming home to oneself that already exists at AfrikaBurn.
