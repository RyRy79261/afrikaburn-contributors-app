# ROAD TO AFRIKABURN 2025: Forefathers Ensemble Fundraiser

Join us on March 30th at Botaki Ba Afrika for an unforgettable day of music, community, and connection as we host a fundraiser to support our return to AfrikaBurn 2025!

By attending this special event, you’ll be contributing to Forefathers Ensemble’s journey to AfrikaBurn, where we’ll share our soul-stirring rhythms with the festival community. Your minimum donation of R100 (or more, if you can!) will help fuel our artistic adventure and bring our music to the dusty plains of AfrikaBurn.

Event Details:

* Date: March 30th

* Venue: Botaki Ba Afrika

* Time: 15h00

* Minimum Donation: R100
