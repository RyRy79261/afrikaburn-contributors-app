## DEAR FELLOW ADVENTURERS,

**Welcome to a world of dazzling soundscapes, beats to move your body and treats to nourish your curiosity, a landscape of desert dreams and wondrous creation.** An oasis to recharge and let go, as shifting sand dunes call us to wander our way home to AfrikaBurn.

**Join us on Friday the 19th at EVOL in Cape Town** for our 8th Day of Creation “Desert Dream II” Party featuring our 8DoC DJs and special guests.

**THEME: Dusty and Dreamy**
