Concept is a massive (17m x15m), fixed-in-place, winged plane designed to land on the dusty expanse of AfrikaBurn. A sound-powered vessel of freedom, music, and magic. The open end of the fuselage will house a DJ booth, while the wings (7.5m long each!) become an interactive dancefloor or performance stage!
