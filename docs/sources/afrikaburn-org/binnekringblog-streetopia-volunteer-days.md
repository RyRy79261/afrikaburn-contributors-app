### Streetopia is an annual event that brings communities and creatives together for a day of colour, creativity and good vibrations. It’s free, and everyone’s invited.

**JOIN US ON THE 15th and 28th of November**  
10H00 - 16H00  
AFRIKABURN HQ, #8 JUNCTION ROAD, SALT RIVER  
Get your hands in on the magic! We're having a creative day for everyone who wants to get involved with STREETOPIA! <https://streetopia.co.za/participate/>

COME MEET THE TEAM! & get in on the GEES!

**What to bring:**  
A snack  
A helpful hand  
A smile on your dial
