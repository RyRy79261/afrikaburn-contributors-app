# AfrikaBurn Board of Members Annual General Meeting 2025

Welcome to the AfrikaBurn AGM – the moment each year when our organisational leaders gather to take stock of the past cycle, and make choices that shape the future. This is where governance happens – the checks and balances on the organisation, event and the movement. The AGM is open to the public, offering anyone a chance to participate.

If you’ve ever wondered what it means to be a member at AfrikaBurn, this is a good place to start. Members are people who choose to take a more active role in the governance of AfrikaBurn – they have the right to vote, nominate directors, and shape how we all participate. So whether you’re a long-time contributor, a curious newcomer, or simply passionate about the burn, welcome. You’re part of the ongoing story of AfrikaBurn – and this is one of the chapters.
