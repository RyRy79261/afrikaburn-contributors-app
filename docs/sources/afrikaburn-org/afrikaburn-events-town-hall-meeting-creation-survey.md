# Town Hall Meeting – CREATION Survey
In this upcoming town hall meeting we’ll be taking you through all the CREATION feedback that we’ve received from those of you who recovered in time to fill in the survey.
Do you want to know how many people were really in Tankwa Town this year?  
Do you want to know if the new supplier policy worked?  
Do you want to know if your CREATION experience was as good as everyone else’s?
Our survey analysis team has been crunching through the numbers trying to figure out what on earth happened this year … and we look forward to sharing the unfiltered(ish) results with you on the 11th of July. We’ll also be broadcasting the Town Hall on our YouTube channel
