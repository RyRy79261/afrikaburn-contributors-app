## Come ignite your inner Dust Ranger and join us for an unforgettable journey at the Johannesburg Botanical Gardens! 🌈🚀
🚨 WHAT TO EXPECT:  
✅ Techniques for Zen-like meditation  
✅ Health and Safety 101 (Safety first, sparkle later!)  
✅ Conflict resolution skills sharper than a cactus  
✅ Approaches to handling situations stickier than Tankwa dust
This isn’t just a training event; it’s a chance to immerse yourself in the magic of AfrikaBurn and become a part of our vibrant community! 🌍✨
🌮 Don’t forget to pack a snack to share, a drink to hydrate, and bring your wildest imagination! 📝✨ And of course, bring your BFF – being a Dust Ranger is more fun with a partner in crime! 🤠👯‍♀️ Also, remember to bring a picnic blanket to sit on and make yourself comfortable during the training session.
**SAVE THE DATE:**  
🗓 Saturday, March 16, 2024  
🕘 9 AM – 1 PM  
📍 Johannesburg Botanical Gardens: Roosevelt high entrance, under the big tree at the bell tower (If you’re lost, call VIV at +27 72 186 4281)
**Please RSVP on the[Facebook event](https://www.facebook.com/events/1933058853755838) or email [tigger@afrikaburn.com](mailto:tigger@afrikaburn.com) to secure your spot! Let’s turn the dust into diamonds together!**
