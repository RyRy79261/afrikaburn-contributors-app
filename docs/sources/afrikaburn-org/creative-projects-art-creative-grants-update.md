## Creative Grants update
  * [ __ Other Author  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/otherauthor/)
  * [ __ November 13, 2012 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2012/11/13/)
  * __ 11:45 am

  * __ Categories: [Art](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/creative-projects/art/)

We’re all flushed with excitement back here at HQ.
The project registrations have been rolling in and Tankwa Town is starting to take shape for 2013.  
There are a lot of creative sparks igniting out there!  
So far 83 projects have registered for AfrikaBurn – Archetypes.  
And 74 of these projects have applied for a creative grant.  
![Creative Grant Update](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/creative-grant-update.jpg)  
We know you’re all champing at the bit for answers and information.  
But please be patient.  
The grant team have their hands full working through these applications, and we’d like the time to do it properly.  
We’re aiming to make the grant announcement around the end of the month.  
In the meantime keep breathing, keep planning, keep working and networking. And keep at it.  
Your project needs you!
## CREATIVE PARTICIPATION
Grant applications are closed, but project registrations are still open.
For those of you still dreaming up ideas, fear not. There is plenty of time to register your creative contribution.
And the good citizens of Tankwa Town would love to see you do your thing on the Binnekring!  
[Head here and register your project](/the-event/participation "Participation") as soon as it’s ready!
