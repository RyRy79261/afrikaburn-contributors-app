**RADICAL SELF-RELIANCE! There are always things that happen in the dust that we do not expect… and that is lots of fun. BUT. HUGE BUT.** There is much that we can plan for NOW:
  * Pack your usual medications – taking a break in the dust is a BAD IDEA and can RUIN YOUR BURN with a SUPER HARD LESSON, even leading to you being taken off site with medical issues – just don’t do it. The burn is not the place to play roulette with your meds. If you are not sure what else your particular meds might mix well/badly with, call into HARM REDUCTION at SANCTUARY 12 noon to 6 pm each day to chat to Luke & Jade.
  * Allow enough water for yourself and those you are travelling with – and then DRINK IT – carry your water bottle EVERYWHERE at burn – you can avoid confusion through dehydration with water intake – alcohol does not hydrate you!
  * Pack warm clothes as well as those skimpy dance numbers – nights can be COLD in the dust!  
**See you there, Sanctuary will help where we can!**
