## Calling all womxn dj’s! ❤
### We want to host you for a set at the Steampunk Saloon at the Burn 2024.
We have a specific vibe at the saloon and our main policy is NO House, techno, or doef doef music. We don’t want to play much electronic music at all, as we’d like to bring some variety to the AB soundscape and there are lots of camps who cater for this style.
**We DO play**  
Swing, Electro Swing, Blues, Funk and Disco, Charleson, Jazz, Rock ‘n Roll, Country, Folk and Acoustic, Soul, Big Band etc…  
We like music with lyrics that once can sing along to and dance the Do Si do…  
Think Happy, Hearty, Sexy, Skippy, Jumpy, Strippy!
**If you play music that fits this bill, please email Lady Magnolia –[rougerevue@gmail.com](mailto:rougerevue@gmail.com) with some examples of the music you’d like to play. ****REPEAT – NO DOEF DOEF!**
