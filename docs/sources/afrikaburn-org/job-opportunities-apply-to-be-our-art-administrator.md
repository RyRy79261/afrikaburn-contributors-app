### The AfrikaBurn Art Portfolio is looking for a full-time Art Administrator who can appreciate a spreadsheet as much as a sunrise in the desert to join our team in Cape Town.

**We’re looking for a young professional with a background in the arts, ready to grow with the ever-evolving work that is to stay relevant and cause impact in the world through community-driven art within the South-African context.**

AfrikaBurn is the strategic driver of [Burning Man](https://burningman.org/) culture in the Southern African region and plays a significant role in the arts and culture sector of South Africa. Most known for its flagship event in the Tankwa Karoo, as a public benefit organisation, AfrikaBurn is the human scaffold that helps a diverse creative community propel numerous arts and development projects. 

## **Key Responsibilities:**

  * **Administrative Support** : 
    * Creative Projects Documentation: Record, track, and follow-up on project registrations;
    * Segment and compile project and artist’s information; 
    * Archive appropriately all portfolio’s related information;
    * Assist with day-to-day operations.
  * **Volunteers Liaison and Coordination** : 
    * Wrangle and coordinate the various Art teams and committees (e.g., ArtCom, Structural Safety, Arteria);
    * Facilitate collaborations between projects and volunteers;
    * Maintain the art volunteers database and knowledge base; 
    * Handle all art portfolio volunteers related matters – Communicating, Calling, Scheduling, Recording, Filing, Assisting, Guiding, etc.
  * **Communications & Reporting**: 
    * Monitor and respond to art-related email correspondence;
    * Ensure the distribution of up-to-date art information across AfrikaBurn’s channels;
    * Take note and flag any publishing of any AfrikaBurn art related content that poses a risk to the AfrikaBurn Ethos and Culture;
    * Contribute to reporting and reflection;
    * Protect the Art and the Artists, and act to ensure fair and equitable radical-self-expression.

## **Requirements:**

  * A diploma or degree in Arts, Humanities, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  * Strong organizational and multitasking skills.
  * Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  * Proficiency in Google Suite and Workspace (specially Sheets and Docs), willingness to experiment with new tools, and system-driven curiosity. 
  * Time and travel flexibility: 
    * Working with volunteers means that often we work weekends and evenings; 
    * Ability to work within an online environment; and a minimum of 3 days a week from our office in Salt River;
    * Availability to travel to the Tankwa Karoo when necessary outside of the annual event time
  * **Bonus Points for:**
    * Have attended the annual AfrikaBurn event in the Tankwa Karoo at least once.
    * Have volunteered at any AfrikaBurn event at least once.
    * Multilingual skills in one or more of the South-African languages, besides English.
    * Driver’s Licence is not mandatory, but it’s useful.

## **How to Apply:**

**Please send your CV, and a cover letter detailing your interest in the position and specific aptitudes for the role to[jobs@afrikaburn.com](mailto:jobs@afrikaburn.com) with a subject title: Art Administrator by Friday 7 February.**

AfrikaBurn is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive environment. All qualified applicants are encouraged to apply.
