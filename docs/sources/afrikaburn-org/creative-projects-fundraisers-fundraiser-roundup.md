## Fundraiser Roundup
  * [ __ TravisLyle  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/travislyle/)
  * [ __ March 19, 2013 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2013/03/19/)
  * __ 11:45 pm

  * __ Categories: [Fundraisers](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/creative-projects/fundraisers/)

With 44 days to go until the San Clan burns, there’s loads happening on the fundraising front! Between now and May 1, we give you the lowdown – here you go!
## **Steampunk Saloon photo sales**
The beautiful belles of the Steampunk Saloon are raising funds by selling postcards, with photos by Anya Kovacs. Think steampunk, think old-time bordello style and you’re on the money. They’re R20 for a pack of 4, and you get to choose which 4 you’d like. Place orders via [femininedivine@neomail.co.za](mailto:femininedivine@neomail.co.za)  
<http://www.facebook.com/pages/The-Steampunk-Saloon-at-Afrikaburn-2013/584793951550599>
## **‘Reflection’ Fundraiser**
‘Reflection’ – it’s one of the biggest AfrikaBurn projects to date, and it’s the brainchild of none other than the legendary Danny Popper – and they’re having a fundraiser this Thursday the 21st March (which is a public holiday!). Dance the day and night away at a rooftop jol featuring an awesome lineup of Bruce Willis, Silo, Tonika, Travis Hyde, Ma’original, Stone Age Citizens, Ctrlroom, David Ireton, Regan and more!  
**When:** Thursday March 21st 1pm – midnight  
**Where:** The Rooftop at 113 Loop Street  
**Entrance:** R60 (and extra donations are always welcome!)  
<http://www.facebook.com/events/359421270840108/>
## **‘Raising The Bats’ Fundraiser**
The crew of the ‘Bat Country Motel’ are gooing a gonzo skop at Carnival Court, 255 Long Street, to help raise funds for our theme camp at AfrikaBurns 2013. The lineup features Drifter (Southerntip-Psy), Compact (Incendia), (Southerntip-Psy), Killawatt (Free Radical Records/Psynopticz) and Nifex (UltraNoise).  
**When:** Saturday March 23rd, 9pm  
**Where:** Carnival Court, Long Street, Cape Town  
**Entrance:** R30 Before 22:00 / R40 After
##### <https://www.facebook.com/events/311072238996274/>
## **Camp Swallowers Of Time Fundraiser**
Get a taste for the offerings by joining this belly dance showcase taking the audience through the roots and routes of belly dance and tribal fusion.  
When: Saturday 23rd March 2013, 7pm till late  
Where: Moyo, Big Bay, Eden on the Bay  
Cost: R140 per person, meal included  
Contact Urshulah for bookings: 083 603 0264 or email [bellydance@urshulah.co.za](mailto:bellydance@urshulah.co.za)
