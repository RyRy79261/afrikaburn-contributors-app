Birthday Suits are run by a group of friends that has been part of this camp for 4 years. Birthday Suits is a naturist camp and give participants the opportunity to share the wonderful world of naturism- a way of life in harmony with nature characterized by the practice of communal nudity with the intention of encouraging self-respect, respect for others and the environment. We create a safe environment for individuals to obtain knowledge of, or first-hand experience in, the naturist environment.  
The Power Shower is our gift- We run a naked human car wash. No funny business, we give you a good and thorough washing. Everyone loves getting out the shower feeling clean and refreshed. And gifting or receiving a wash is a beautiful and respectful experience.  
Participants in the Power Shower enjoy a naturist experience by queuing naked for the Power Shower and receiving a warm, soapy wash is wonderful.  
We gift cold orange juice during shower hour  
Birthday Suits would like to open our space in the afternoon and evening for others to share their poetry, storytelling or art workshops
