# Le Petit Paris

Le Petit Paris : Tribute to the famous Cape Town bar called Le Petit Paris.

Our project has been the same since the creation of this camp: share moments with human beings, offer gift, and to celebrate the sunset loaded with emotions, love and pleasure of the present time. Over the years, we have improved our Eiffel Tower, the organization of our camp, and our creative ideas.
