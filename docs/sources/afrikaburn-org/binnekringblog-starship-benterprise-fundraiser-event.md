## Leap! Aboard the Starship Benterprise for a journey through time and space!
**Expect the unexpected as creatures from all across the galaxies convene at this mind boggling lunar saloon, filled with intergalactic musicians, djs, puppeteers and black light landscapes.** One night only, a feast for the minds eyes…
Venue: [78a Avenue, 4th Ave, Melville, Johannesburg, 2092](https://www.instagram.com/smokingkillsbar/?hl=en)  
March 1st from 6pm.  
Donations from R80
**Dress up encouraged, kooky wookies welcomed. An Afrikaburn artists fundraiser, to get the best in the west to the dust or bust. Bleep.**
**The Puppetorium is an inclusive venture co created with Anathi ticket holders in a shared upskilling adventure to the desert.**
