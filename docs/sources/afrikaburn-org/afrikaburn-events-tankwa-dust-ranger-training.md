# Tankwa Dust Ranger Training
**Ready to see the world _Through the Prism_?**
This is your chance to explore the many colourful facets of Rangering — where empathy meets action, and every shade of experience helps us shine brighter together.
Whether you’re brand new and curious about those orange-clad legends, or a seasoned Ranger looking to refresh your skills, this training session is for you. Join us for a morning of learning, laughter, and a little Tankwa magic.
_**What to Expect**_
💫 **The 11 Guiding Principles, refracted** – how Rangers live and embody each principle in real-world situations  
🚑 **Safety & preparedness** – practical tools for caring for yourself and others, in the dust and beyond  
🤝 **Conflict navigation with heart** – bringing calm, clarity, and compassion when things get murky  
🔦 **Through the Prism moments** – creative ways to respond when the unexpected shows up
🎒 _**What to Bring**_
🌞 Snacks to eat _and_ share — generosity fuels connection  
💧 Something to sip — hydration is happiness  
🖊️ A pen and notebook (or colourful crayons, if inspiration strikes)  
🧡 A friend who’s curious about Rangering — everything’s better shared
📍 _**Event Details**_
📅 **Saturday, 24 January 2026**  
⏰ **09:00 – 13:00**  
📍 **AfrikaBurn HQ** , 8 Junction Rd, Salt River, Cape Town  
📞 **RSVP / Info:** Ranger Bob — jono@afrikaburn.com
Come ready to learn, connect, and let your unique light shine **Through the Prism**.  
See you there, future legends of the dust. 🌈✨
