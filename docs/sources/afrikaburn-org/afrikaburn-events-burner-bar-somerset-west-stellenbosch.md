# Burner Bar – Somerset West & Stellenbosch

Somerset West & Stellenbosch Burner Bar! Come and join us for a lekker evening with DJ Paul on the turn tables, great food and a wide variety of beer.  
Burner Bar is a fully participant-run event, if you want to create one in your area, please go ahead. Burner Bar is always looking for people to jump in and help organise, just give us a shout if you are keen. It is intended to be purely social, with no agenda.
