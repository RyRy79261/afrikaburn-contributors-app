## Disconnect to reconnect

**This year, we’re inviting all the theme camps, support camps and every camp in between to choose not to provide internet access to their theme camp members or other participants during the 7 event days and nights.**

If you wish to bring an internet connection for your own, private use, no problem… this is about not beaming it around and allowing other people to jump on it.

By keeping our camps and spaces offline, we aren’t just “unplugging”; we are protecting the magic of AfrikaBurn for everyone.

**Let’s choose to be here, now, together.**
