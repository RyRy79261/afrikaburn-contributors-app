**Join The Giant Sand Worm Collective for an intimate event in the Bokaap.**
We will be swimming in vibrations of a piano sound journey by Jeffrey Munks before he picks it up into a melodic techno live set. This is sure to be quite the sonic experience!
DJ Phanny & Watching Love will make sure the vinyl are spinning when you arrive & before you leave.
**Doors open at 6pm and there will be bar with all the usual suspects. Since this is a fundraiser event for The Giant Sandworm Collective, entrance is donation-based starting at R50.**
Grab your tickets at the door or through the link in below. (just make sure to put ‘Wormhole’ as the reference).
<https://pay.yoco.com/thateclectic>
**A little more about The Giant Sand Worm Collective:**  
Personalization, roleplay, improv, discomfort, triggers, immersion, play, self reflection, vulnerability…. These are some of things we try to embody at the giant sand worm.
**Whether you choose to play as yourself or someone else, there is no right or wrong**
