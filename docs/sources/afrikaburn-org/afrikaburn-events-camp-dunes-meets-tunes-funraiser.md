# Camp Dunes Meets Tunes Funraiser
Experience a sprinkling of fairy dust from the most magical camp on the Binnekring, Dunes Meets Tunes, at our fundraiser on *Friday 21 March*. From a symphony of strings to dirty basslines to euphoric synths, all punctuated by the beat of the djembe, the lineup is a journey unto itself ![🎻](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/16.0/1f3bb/32.png) ![🎧](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/16.0/1f3a7/32.png) ![🪘](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/16.0/1fa98/32.png) See you at 70a Kloof St. Doors open at 6pm with the party continuing into the early hours of the morning. A donation of R150 is payable at the door. With love DMT xx
