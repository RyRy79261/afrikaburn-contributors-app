## Pangea Project Theme camp fundraiser in London UK

**One night only – The Pangea Project is bringing that African Heat to East London.** Warm your body on the dance floor with epic Afrobeats, Afrohouse, and Amapiano … They don’t call it a burn for nothing.
**Dress code:** RAINBOW / Represent your nation / African Print. We would love for you to be part of our vision and experience the Pangea Project vibe.
**Please join us on March 24th at Metropolis London (UK)** to get your feet sandy for a heated night of dancing, drinks and delightful people as our camp members play some of their favourite African tunes.
**Date Friday 24th March**
**Time: 20:30 until 03:00**
**Location: Metropolis, Bethnal Green: 234 Cambridge Heath Rd, London E2 9NN**
Tickets: <https://billetto.co.uk/e/rainbow-nation-elevation-fundraiser-tickets-789617>
