## AfrikaBurn 2023 registrations are now open!

  * [ __ The Brain  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/brain/)
  * [ __ September 13, 2022 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2022/09/13/)
  * __ 11:14 am

  * __ Categories: [Creative Projects](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/creative-projects/), [Latest News](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/latest-news/)

![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Steven-Morrow-SM_20220425_28084_FB-1.jpg)

  * __ Image credit: Steven Morrow, Art: Dragon Mania 

**The AfrikaBurn Tribe system has just had its[memory wiped](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWgrvNHjKkY) and is running around the office screaming something about being married to Sharon Stone and there being oxygen on Mars?** What this means for us earthlings is all the AfrikaBurn 2023 creative project registrations are now open.

If you’re planning on taking a trip into [Space](https://tickets.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-2023-theme/) next year and have been inspired to participate by bringing along an artwork, a performance, a theme camp or mutant vehicle, then this is the start of your countdown to launch on the 24th April 2023.

**Register yourself (and your project!) by clicking here:<https://tribe.afrikaburn.com/>**

**![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Steven-Morrow-B2SM_20220430_28716_FB-1.jpg)It doesn’t matter if you’re from the planet Warhol or Kahlo, anyone and everyone is invited to create art at AfrikaBurn.**

All it takes is an idea, and the will to make it happen. Collaborate or don’t, whether you’re a pro or not, it’s simply about self-expression. Go as big or as small as you like: the only limits are your imagination.

[Find out more about Art & Performance by clicking here. ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/event/participation/artworks-performances/)
