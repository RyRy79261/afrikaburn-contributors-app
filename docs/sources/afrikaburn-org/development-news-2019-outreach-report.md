Presenting our [**AfrikaBurn Outreach Report for 2019**](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Outreach-FY19.pdf). This past year has been an incredible journey of collaboration and connection with the communities surrounding the Tankwa Karoo. In these pages, you’ll discover the projects and partnerships we’ve embarked upon, all reflecting our commitment to social responsibility and community development. From educational programs and art workshops to environmental conservation efforts, we’re proud to share how we bring our core principles—like inclusion, participation, and civic responsibility—beyond the dust and into the everyday lives of our neighbors.
