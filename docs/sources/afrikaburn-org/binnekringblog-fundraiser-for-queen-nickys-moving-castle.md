**A moving, breathing castle, held together by magic and moon dust!**
  * Will you see her huffing along the desert horizon?
  * Or will you be abducted by the creatures lurking in her depths?
  * Will you meet her fire demon?
  * And will you boogie on her doorsteps?

We hope that it is all of the above! And when you are reeling from her magic moonbeams back in the default world, come celebrate her by kicking up the dust again **on the 7th of May at Saggy Stone.**
We will have DJs playing some Downtempo, Techno and Psy tunes, as well as an open deck! Do you have some material you would like to gift? Come play with us! **Entrance is R80 and we would love to see all of you beautiful burny babes there!**
Travel safe there and back, and look after each other. In Dust we Trust.
