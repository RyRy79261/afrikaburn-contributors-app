## On the 21st of March, **8DoC is bringing an aurora of burn magic to Berlin to warm you guys up for the great burn in April.**
**We’re raising funds for our project in the desert: the big pregnant lady, the rebirthing arena, the womb, the giant vagina or whatever you may call her. To give you burners the most amazing time in the desert, we’re going to do a fundraiser in Berlin to gather the funds to do so.**
Call all your friends in Berlin, let them know about this amazing party and help us throw the most amazing party in the dust. This fundraiser will be no ordinary party, expect top-notch sound from the DJs that will cherish your ears in the desert as well, a fire show, a comedy show, a market, freshly baked sourdough pizza and a lot more burn magic from the amazing 8DoC crew. This will be a night that will echo through your memory; it will be the light through your prism. This is “Prismatic Dreams”.
**Check our Instagram for details on the party:** [instagram.com/8doc.art](http://instagram.com/8doc.art)
Artist applications are also still open. Do you want to share your magic with us? Get in touch through the link in our bio. **See you in the dust!**
