# Supporting Our Desert Hydration Dream

## I’m excited to share with you an opportunity to support Hydration Station, a theme camp dedicated to quenching the thirst of Burn participants in the desert.

As an artist/designer, I’ve been crafting custom stretch tents for a hydration station for 9 years, and last year opened this up to the wider Burn community to help fund the hydration station with logistics of getting to the burn.

***Why Buy?***
– Lightweight & transportable by car
– UV resistant, Waterproof, Wind flexible
– Only R180 per square meter
– Largest size: 150m²

Your purchase supports Hydration Station’s mission to hydrate participants and donate reusable water containers to local communities.

***Hydration Station’s Cycle of Life:***
1. Create awesome stretchies to fund our theme camp
2. Volunteers donate 20L of clean water in reusable containers
3. Containers are donated to local communities for water storage in their homes.

We’re passionate about partying with purpose and would love your support.
