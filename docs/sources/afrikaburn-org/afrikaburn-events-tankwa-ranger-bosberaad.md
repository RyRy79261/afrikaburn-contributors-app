# Tankwa Ranger Bosberaad

📣🔥 Tankwa Rangers! Suit up for a Ranger Rendezvous! 🔥🎩

Gather ‘round, Rangers of the Burn! For you are being summoned to an epic workshop where we’ll hash out all things Rangerly and conjure our plans for the next AfrikaBurn Out of the Blue 2025. This is your chance to bring your brightest ideas, wildest dreams, and all the gees you can muster!

🗺️ Venue: Hide-away Lodge, Blombosch Game Farm, Ysterfontein  
📅 Date: 24 August  
⏰ Time: 9:30 am until we burn out (around 5pm-ish)

Some highlights of the day will include cooking up a strategy for 2025/6 and crafting a sizzling action plan. We’ll deep dive into sessions covering the various portfolios and have specific discussions about our AfrikaBurn community’s 11 principles!

Whether you’re an old flame or a fresh spark, your presence is essential. Let’s brainstorm, scheme, and prepare to make the next AfrikaBurn a blazing success!

Please RSVP to phoenix@afrikaburn.com so that we can ensure you are kept up to date with all the info around the day.

🔥✨💡

P.S. No fish were harmed in the making of this workshop… but plenty of puns were!
