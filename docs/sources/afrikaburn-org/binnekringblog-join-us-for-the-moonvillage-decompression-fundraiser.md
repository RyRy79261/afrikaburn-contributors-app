## Greetings AfrikaBurners!
**We’re excited to invite you to the MoonVillage Decompression Fundraiser, an event to bring our vibrant community together and support a great cause.** As a small emerging theme camp, we are thrilled to have Amy Mauritz on deck to massage our brains with her deep house music. Plus, our lineup includes other fantastic DJs who will keep the beats going all night long!
## Date: 15th June  
Time: To Be Confirmed  
Location: 196 Victoria, Woodstock
Indulge in delicious Plushi (plant based sushi) and enjoy drinks from the bar while you dance and connect with fellow Burners. All extra revenue from this fundraiser will be donated to charities, so your good vibes will spread even further!
**For more info and updates, follow us on Instagram @zemoonvillage. Let’s make this a night to remember and support a brighter future together. See you there!**
