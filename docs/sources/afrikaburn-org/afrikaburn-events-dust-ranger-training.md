# DUST RANGER TRAINING
Step into the vibrant world of Rangering — a place where empathy turns into action, and every unique perspective adds another beam to the spectrum. This training is an open invitation to explore what it really means to Ranger, whether you’re newly curious about those orange-clad legends or a seasoned Ranger keen to reconnect, refine, and recharge your skills.
Join us for a morning infused with learning, laughter, and a generous dose of Tankwa magic.
✨ What to Expect:  
💫 The 11 Guiding Principles, through a Ranger lens — how we live, interpret, and apply them in real-life moments  
🚑 Safety & preparedness — practical ways to care for yourself and others, in the dust and beyond  
🤝 Navigating conflict with heart — cultivating calm, clarity, and compassion when things get tricky  
🔦 Through the Prism moments — creative, grounded responses when the unexpected arrives
🎒 What to Bring:  
🌞 Snacks to share — generosity sparks connection  
💧 Something to sip — hydration is essential  
🖊️ A pen and notebook (or colourful crayons, if that’s your style)  
🧡 A friend who’s curious about Rangering — everything’s better together
📍 Event Details:  
📅 Saturday, 7 February 2026  
⏰ 09:00 – 13:00  
📍 AfrikaBurn HQ, 8 Junction Road, Salt River, Cape Town
For more info, contact Ranger Sunshine on WhatsApp: 083 231 4723
