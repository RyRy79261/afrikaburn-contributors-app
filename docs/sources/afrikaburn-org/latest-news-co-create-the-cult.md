Have bold philosophical ideas you’d love to bring to AfrikaBurn governance?  
Enjoy late-evening meetings, spirited debates, and helping shape the future of the organisation?
Afrikaburn Membership is calling for fresh, dedicated candidates to join the Membership. The Membership Candidacy is a 9 month program where you will be introduced to organisation governance and the work that is Membership! 
Please note: this is a fully volunteer-based group – passion highly encouraged.
Application requires a short questionnaire, your CV, and a recommendation from someone in the AB community (anyone who has attended an AfrikaBurn event and knows your passion). Application pack is here: [Applicants Submission Pack – Membership](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1S1L7cj9QdSBhhgQUFQ66oilrk6YcfG12?usp=drive_link)
Contact Memsec@afrikaburn.com if you have any questions.
Step up, lean in, and help craft the next chapter.
Find out more: <https://www.afrikaburn.org/the-organisation/about/governance-and-transparency/>
