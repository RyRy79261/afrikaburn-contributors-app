## Our fundraising event was a massive hit!

**Sadly, we did not make much money from it and the crowdfunding campaign which we hoped to spread with the event went for the most part unnoticed.** If we don’t hit our crowdfunding goal, we do not get any of the funds everyone has already pledged to it.

**We have two weeks. Help us reach our goal, and you will be rewarded with hugs, kisses, and so many lovely times in the belly of our soon-to-be creation of peace and unity.**
