# HEARTSPACE presents: SUN SPLASH SOUL: A MINI-RETREAT & POOL PARTY

HEARTSPACE presents:

SUN SPLASH SOUL: A MINI-RETREAT & POOL PARTY

Join us high above Cape Town for a soul-shaking, sun-soaked half-day retreat at Lighthouse Studio, where wellness meets playfulness in true AfrikaBurn spirit!

Start your day with soulful yoga, juicy breathwork, and mind-expanding workshops, then let loose with dancing and beats from our rooftop DJ. Expect yoga flows, meditation magic, and a community of like-minded legends—all with a 360° view of the Mother City.

And when you’re blissed out and ready to float? Dive into the pool party for golden-hour swims, fresh beats, and deliciously good vibes.

Featuring Lara Roux, Margarita Celeste, Kirsty, Persephone and more!

TICKET OPTIONS:  
– Mini-Retreat + Pool Party – The full experience! Yoga, workshops, meditation, movement, and then straight into the poolside beats.  
– Pool Party Pass – Skip the zen, go straight to the splashy, sun-drenched fun.

This is a fundraiser for Heart Space at AfrikaBurn, so come shake, soak, and celebrate with us—all for a cause that’s pure magic.

30 March | 11h00 – 17h00 | Lighthouse Studio, Cape Town  
Spaces are limited—grab your ticket now and let’s make rooftop memories!
