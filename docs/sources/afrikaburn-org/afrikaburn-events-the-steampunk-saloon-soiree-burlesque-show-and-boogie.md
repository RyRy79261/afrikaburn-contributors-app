# The Steampunk Saloon Soirée – Burlesque Show and Boogie

## Dust off your finest corsets, top hats, and goggles because we’re taking you on a dusty frolic.

**Brace yourselves for scintillating burlesque performances by our brazen steampunk sirens and thereafter, kick off your shoes and have a dusty stomp to our finest Saloon DJ’s, playing songs with lyrics!**

- Saturday 28th February 2026
- The Electric, Cape Town
- Doors open at 7pm
- Performances start at 8pm so don’t be late!
- Tickets R185

**Bring your dancing shoes as we are going to finish off the night with a serious boogie!**
