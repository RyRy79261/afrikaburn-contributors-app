Participants needed:
Our super-hero hosts of camp ABnB comprises of a wide range of regulars and virgins from around the world. Crew members main contribution will be their time, effort and energy. Everyone is allocated responsibilities that include general camp stuff, gifting, and general hosting to help burners feel even more welcomed. 
## Project Description:
Nestled in the dust you will find a safe haven  
for those of you looking to chill and connect.  
Equipped with a large lounge area, cocktail bar, mutant  
and some deep soft-ish tunes. You may  
want to stay for an hour, a night or a day;  
the choice is yours.
![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IMG-20230504-WA0002-8a7c7e8b54745154ebe6c89ae3b35c88-1024x771.jpg)
## Details of Gift:
The theme camp space is open 24/7 from Monday till Sunday.
A bar serving adult beverages will run from 2pm-ish till the daily quota of adult ingredients runs out. This is sometimes paired with a DJ.
We have the ABnB Rover (mini mutant) that will often operate in the evenings. It serves warm adult beverages. The rover does occasionally leave the safety of the camp and head out on the playa for adventures.
The large well furnished space includes games.
In collaboration with other burners we make our space available to host their gifts/events.
## Joining Requirements:
Come early to build, and/or stay late to packup. Works shifts to keep our home running so we can focus on the loving.

The Crossing consists of two groupings of elements. A rigid line cuts through the landscape, a boundary that may represent law, structure, or control. Intersecting this line are more organic forms that rise irregularly, as though shaped by wind, erosion, or time. The line may be read as a border or a threshold, a boundary between order and chaos, captivity and freedom, life and death. Movement unfolds across this divide. From one perspective, the passage across the line could represent departure or exile, figures leaving something familiar behind. From another, the same movement may suggest arrival, a transition toward possibility and renewal. The Crossing comes together through shared effort, people learning, experimenting, problem-solving, and physically constructing something at scale. It exists because of a community, for a short time, shaped by the desert and the people within it. As with many desert installations, the sculpture is intended to transform through fire. When it ultimately burns, the work completes its own crossing, shifting from structure into flame, light, and ash.
