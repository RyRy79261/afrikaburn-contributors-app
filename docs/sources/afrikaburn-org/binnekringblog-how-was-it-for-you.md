**How much fun was that? Did you jump over the moon with joy? Did you trip the light fandango? Was the vacuum of SPACE a lonely place or the only place in the universe that you want to be?**

While it’s still fresh in your mind, and before it’s all erased by the default world experience, we’d love to know what you thought of your AfrikaBurn experience this year. It takes about 30 minutes, which might feel like a light year online - but is well worth it.
