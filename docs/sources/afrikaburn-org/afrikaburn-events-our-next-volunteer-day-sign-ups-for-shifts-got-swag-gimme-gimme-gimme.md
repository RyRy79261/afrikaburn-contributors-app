# Our Next Volunteer Day / Sign Ups for Shifts / Got SWAG? Gimme Gimme Gimme
Hey hey!
Our next Volunteer Day is coming up on **Saturday the 29th of March**  
**10am to 4pm**  
**#8 Junction Road Salt River**
We’ll be tie – tying t -shirts, painting DMV flags, sewing for ARTeria, making Chillaz Decor and much much more!
FYI – RFT is in the house! The pirates are back in full swing
Shout out to Esme for filling our tummies and tantalizing our tastebuds with the most delicious lunch! As always!
…. and special thanks to the MO & TRIBE for doing a musical mash up / take-over at our last volunteer day!  
They will be back! So bring your voices, your instrumental jams and your dance moves !!! While we cut some shapes (literally) and groove to some beats.
We want to see you there!
We also want to see you [SIGN UP FOR SHIFTS](https://signup.com/Group/9725392720114/)  
Click the link: https://signup.com/Group/9725392720114/
There ain’t no party like an old school party….  
& the best way to hang with some Afrika Burn OGs is by picking up a volunteer shift  
We will all chip in =)
AND FINALLY!
GOT SWAG? This is a call out!
We want to custom make some Volunteer Swag by upcycling the old into the new!
Got any PLAIN t-shirts, jeans, skirts, bell-bottoms, dresses, shorts? Anything that will take to a screen for some screen printing? ZERO branding! Zero texture!
That’s right! Your new and improved SWAG is on its way! So please drop off any old clothing that fits the description above at the Afrika Burn HQ before the 26th of March.
**What is SWAG? Come to our Volunteer Day and Sign up for a Shift – you’ll find out!**
