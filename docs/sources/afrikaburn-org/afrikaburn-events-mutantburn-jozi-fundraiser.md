# MutantBurn Jozi Fundraiser

## MutantBurn Jozi is a fundraiser event brought to you by four Johannesburg-based AfrikaBurn camps: Lick & Dab, Garden of Weeden, Boomicorn, and The Dung Beetle!

**Kicking off at 11am with a ceremony to open the Boma dancefloor, the event promises non-stop fun with two dance floors featuring DJs and a Mutant Vehicle link-up by The Dung Beetle and Boomicorn and other MUTANT VEHICLES that you’ll recognise, creating a visual phenomenon, transforming the venue into a playground of creativity and wonder.**

There will be two full-service bars plus a dedicated tequila bar to keep the good vibes flowing and an array of delicious food and other goodies on sale at the mini market.

Join them for a day of music, dance, and community as they invite you to come together to support a great cause.

*A percentage of ticket sales will be donated to the charities chosen by our camps*

### ***LIMITED TICKETS AVAILABLE @ THE DOOR***

**IMPORTANT NOTICE:**

**LIMITED PARKING**  
Due to extremely limited parking, we highly recommend using Uber or other services to get to the venue comfortably and hassle-free.

**NO OUTSIDE DRINKS ALLOWED**  
To ensure the safety and enjoyment of all attendees, please be aware that no outside drinks are permitted to be brought into the event venue. All bags will be checked upon entry, and any drinks found will be confiscated.

**R.O.A.R (Right of Admission Reserved)**  
The event organisers reserve the right to refuse entry or remove any individual who does not comply with this policy or any other event regulations.
