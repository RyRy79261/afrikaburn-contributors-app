**We’re beginning our quest to Quaggafontein and SPACE with our first volunteer day of the year … ALL SYSTEMS GO!** This is a great way to meet the people who make things happen, learn who’s who in the zoo, catch up with old friends and get to know the community. We love watching our community grow and AfrikaBurn HQ is the place to be! **Join us at the Junction Hotel this coming Saturday from 10 am to 4 pm.**
We’ll be assisting Sanctuary, mingling with DMV, marking flags and painting signs with the Volunteer Leads. To help us provide you with a hearty meal, please be sure to register on our [Facebook page](https://fb.me/e/6i0KRlecd) or RSVP at volunteers@afrikaburn.com.
