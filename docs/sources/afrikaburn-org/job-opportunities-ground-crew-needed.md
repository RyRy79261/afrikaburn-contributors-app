**AfrikaBurn is seeking an Asset and Yard Coordinator to work as a part of its ground crew at the headquarters Junction Hotel in Salt River, Cape Town. While some might think the ABHQ looks like a bomb went off in an art gallery, it’s also the space where we keep our materials and resources in between burns, as well as host volunteer days, workshops and events.** We need a diligent and dedicated person to keep it shipshape, who can see how important that rusty old caravan wheel is for a project, and make sure it gets to the right place at the right time in the right condition.

AfrikaBurn is the strategic driver of Burning Man culture in the Southern African region and plays a significant role in the arts and culture sector of South Africa. Most known for its flagship event in the Tankwa Karoo, as a public benefit organisation, AfrikaBurn is the human scaffold that helps a diverse creative community propel numerous arts and development projects.

**Responsibilities**

  * **Coordinate the yard at Junction Hotel as an active, multi use space:**  
Keep the yard neat and orderly  
Coordinate and maintain the storage facilities and parking bays  
Control access to the yard, and the security of the yard.  
Run the budget associated with yard maintenance and usage  
Coordinate the online booking system  
Set up spaces and equipment  
Support portfolio activities in the yard  
Promote volunteerism and run yard days throughout the year  
Coordinate the yard lessees.  
Ensure implementation of policies related to yard usage  
Establish and maintain a preventative maintenance schedule

  * **Coordinate the maintenance, usage and registry of AfrikaBurn’s assets:**  
Service, maintain and repair AfrikaBurns assets so that they are available in good working condition and are safe to use.  
Maintain an up-to-date registry, insurance of assets, licensing and compliance of assets  
Track movements of assets between the Junction Hotel, Quaggafontein and other locations.  
Assist and support AfrikaBurn portfolios in the use of its various assets  
Ensure implementation of all policies related to asset usage
