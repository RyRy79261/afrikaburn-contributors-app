## CITIZENS OF TANKWA TOWN, IT’S TIME TO GATHER!

Gather our wits, gather our friends and our finest costume – it’s time to reconnect and release the pressure. Get ready to dress up and get down – it’s Decompression time!

Thanks to a great showing from you, our community, we have a massive number of activities taking place:  
3 STAGES – MUTANT VEHICLES – SKY DANCERS – BURLESQUE – THE CARDINAL CAROUSEL – CURIOUS GIRAFFE – FIRE PERFORMANCE – CYR WHEEL – AND YOU!

**WHAT & WHEN**  
Date: 27th July  
Time: 7pm – late  
Venue: Salt River Studios, Voortrekker Rd, Cape Town  
Tickets: R100 pre-sales via Quicket, R120 on the door  
Dress: Come in your finest Tankwa finery!

**TICKETS**  
ARE LIMITED TO 1200 – GET THEM ON QUICKET: [www.quicket.co.za/events/2343-decompression/](http://www.quicket.co.za/events/2343-decompression/)  
Funds raised will go toward AfrikaBurn Outreach – there’ll also be boxes on the night for donations of tinned food, clothing and blankets.

**DIRECTIONS:**  
From Salt River Circle, go over the bridge toward Maitland, at the end of the bridge turn left and follow the signs.

**WANT TO GET INVOLVED?**  
Keen to help out? Let us know how you’d like to get involved by using the Volunteer form on our site:  
[www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/volunteer-now](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/volunteer-now)

And if you’re keen to bring your special piece of Tankwa art along, email specialevents@afrikaburn.com

Thanks to all who have volunteered so far, you rock our world (and our Decompression)!

**PLEASE NOTE:** No under 18s – and no MOOP please folks!
