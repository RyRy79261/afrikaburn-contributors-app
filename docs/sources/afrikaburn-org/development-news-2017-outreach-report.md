Hold onto your hats and dust goggles – here’s the [**AfrikaBurn 2017 Outreach Report**](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Outreach-FY17-REPORT.pdf)! This year, we’ve focused on extending our efforts beyond the event, engaging with neighboring communities through art, education, and sustainability projects. From supporting local schools to building meaningful connections, we’ve aimed to make a positive impact. In this report, you’ll find an overview of our initiatives and the collaborative work that made them possible. Thank you for being part of this journey with us.
