# Piss Poor Priscilla’s Pittea Party

## The Monarchy has run into some (ahem) financial difficulties. The offshore accounts have run dry and the people are uprising. So we have decided to let them drink tea. And you, dear commoner, are cordially invited.

**There will be high tea at 3pm,** traders selling their bonny wares, and a 358 year French vampire in drag bringing you some tunes when the sun goes down. Please do come. Or it’s off with your head. You can follow our bacchanalian endeavours and get updates from [@priscilla.qotd](https://www.instagram.com/priscilla.qotd/)

### **_Date:_** 27th of July

### **_Time:_** 3pm to midnight

### **_Location:_** Touch of Madness, Observatory, 12 Nuttall Road
