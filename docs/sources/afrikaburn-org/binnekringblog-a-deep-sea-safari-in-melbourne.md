## One of Australia’s longest running and most loved theme camps, Kamp Kraken, is headed to Afrika Burn this year – and taking everyone on an underwater safari to get there. Gear up in your best aquatic gear and dive into the unknown. This isn’t just a fundraiser; it’s a descent into a world where the rules of the surface don’t apply. Whether you’re cloaked in pelagic shadows or shimmering in the coral shallows, this one’s for anyone in Melbourne who’s looking for a great Saturday out.
Expect heart-pounding beats from our DJs, immersive decor that transports you to the ocean floor, hypnotic visuals, and performances that push the boundaries. This is the underground ocean party of the year, and it’s all in aid of getting us to the Tankwa Karoo Desert this April.
**Where: Melbourne (Australia)**  
**When: 16 March 2024**  
**Tickets:<https://www.facebook.com/events/354433884111316/>**
