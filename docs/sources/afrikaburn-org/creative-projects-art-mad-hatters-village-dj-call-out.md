![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/MadHatters-logo-preview-1-b1033ae0b603ee64cb77ace44fcc1912.jpg)

  * __ Image credit: Provided 

The Mad Hatters Village will be curating DJ sets both at the Mad Hatters Lounge and on our Sound Mutant from Monday to Saturday of Burn week.

The lounge has a more intimate 4K sound system, loud enough to play to a smaller audience at a beautiful destination with views of the mountains.

The Sound Mutant has a powerful sound system that can play to a large crowd, it will entertain people at the Mad Hatters Lounge for our Sunset parties from 4 till 9pm each night at 11-ish Street and E, tucked in alongside the dunes with magical views of the sunset and over Tankwa Town and later can be seen parked alongside a sculpture on the Binnekring or deep playa entertaining the mad at heart.

If you are interested in playing on one of these two platforms, please fill in your information on the following DJ Sign up form – 

https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdBO3pekTP4cpascmMKWKtO7D1ydEPoC6K0UwagE5MwIKeCOA/viewform

Our Sound leads programming each day of the Burn may be in touch. We do believe in looking after the DJs that are camping in the Mad Hatters Village first, but there are plenty of slots, so you might get lucky.
