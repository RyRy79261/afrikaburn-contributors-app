# MutantBurn Jozi: 2nd Edition

![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/MBJ_Feb_2025-1e5d170f8092c841946bb53375eb8c48.jpg)
# MutantBurn Jozi: 2nd Edition
We’re back for the 2nd Edition of MutantBurn Jozi, brought to you by your favorite Johannesburg-based AfrikaBurn camps: Lick & Dab and Garden of Weeden. Prepare for an unforgettable day as we take everything you loved about the first event and turn up the heat.
Starting at 4pm, the festivities kick off with an electrifying opening ceremony at the Boma dancefloor, setting the tone for a day packed with magic, music, and mayhem. This year, we’ve expanded the experience with three pumping dancefloors, featuring an incredible lineup of AfrikaBurn DJs to keep you moving.
Get ready for a visual spectacle as the Mutant Vehicles light up the venue with their dazzling creativity, transforming the space into an epic playground of imagination.
**Full-Service Bars: including a dedicated tequila bar for those extra spicy vibes.**
**Mini Market: Treat yourself to a variety of delicious food options and shop for unique goodies from local vendors.**
**Creativity & Community: Immerse yourself in the artistry, energy, and connection that makes this event one-of-a-kind.**
This isn’t just a party—it’s a way to give back! A percentage of ticket sales will go to charities chosen by our camps, ensuring that the spirit of gifting and generosity thrives.
Important Info  
Limited Tickets: Available at the door—don’t miss out!  
Cashless Event: All transactions are cashless for your convenience.
Venue Policies  
**Limited Parking: Skip the stress—use Uber or rideshare services.**
**No Outside Drinks: Bags will be checked at the gate to ensure everyone’s safety and fun.**
**R.O.A.R (Right of Admission Reserved): We reserve the right to refuse entry or remove individuals who don’t follow our guidelines.**
**No Under 23s | No Kids: This event is strictly for adults only.**
Join us for a day of music, dance, and togetherness, where art, creativity, and community collide. We can’t wait to create magic with you!
Let’s make the 2nd Edition of MutantBurn Jozi even bigger, better, and brighter than ever. See you there!
