**AfrikaBurn is seeking a Theme Camp Coordinator to join their team in Cape Town and wrangle all the things Theme Camps, for its events, activities and projects.**

**Often at the coalface of community matters, this facilitative role sits within the Creative Portfolio and is the link between the volunteer Theme Camp Committee, volunteer Theme Camp Leads and the Operations team, it principally guides humans towards curating and creating a beautiful and engaging Tankwa Town.**

As a people-facing part-time position with variable demands throughout the year, the Theme Camp Coordinator is required to work in various locations and work outside of standard office hours. Work is primarily sedentary in nature in town with no special demand. In the desert, however, work is of a physical nature, sometimes in adverse weather conditions.

**Key responsibilities include:  
**Supporting the registration process for Theme Camp submissions  
Reviewing and overseeing Theme Camp allocations  
Placement and mapping of Theme Camps in Tankwa Town  
Committee and community management  
Public facing communications  
Implementing and upholding policy  
Fielding enquiries and facilitating creative projects and collaborations

Your required skills and competencies will be:  
Proficiency in Microsoft Word and Excel as a minimum or Google Docs and Sheets  
Project management – moving multiple projects forward  
Data and trend mapping and analysis  
Aptitude for online communications FaceBook, email and other platforms  
Presentation and people skills  
Sound understanding of Burn culture and the ethos of AfrikaBurn.  
Teamwork, diplomacy and a service mentality  
Confidence when tackling unforeseen or sensitive issues  
Able to manage tight deadlines and needs of different role players  
Self-motivation and flexibility  
Driver’s license and preferably own transport

**Your preferred attributes are:**  
Previous participation in AfrikaBurn especially in Theme Camp function  
Experience in facilitation, mediation or community organizing  
Experience leading team or group  
Experience with volunteer recruitment, training and retention.

If you believe you could respond to our unique requirements, please apply to [jobs@afrikaburn.com](mailto:jobs@afrikaburn.com) with a subject title: Theme Camp Coordinator, providing a CV and a letter of motivation. AfrikaBurn reserves the right not to make this appointment. **Deadline for applications has now closed.**
