**![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Bobman.jpg)Lockdown Champions**

When ‘in person’ or ‘face to face’ arts and culture went into freefall during the pandemic, the AfrikaBurn community stepped up to the spinning plates. Along with some relief funding opportunities, and some incredibly talented individuals in our volunteer committees, the organisation pulled through. Thank you to the donors during this period who kept us able to invent the world anew.
