**Somerset West & Stellenbosch Burner Bar!** Come and join us for a lekker evening with DJ Paulie on the turn tables, great food and a wide variety of drinks.
A Burner Bar is a fully participant-run event, if you want to create one in your area, please go ahead. Burner Bar is always looking for people to jump in and help organise, just give us a shout if you are keen. It is intended to be purely social, with no agenda.
**Date: 18:30 – Fri 9 Feb**  
**Location:****Triggerfish Brewing**  
**W.R Quinan Blvd Unit 9, Somerset West**
