## Introducing ‘The Crossing’, an AfrikaBurn art installation created by Nathan V Honey and you.
**Nathan has been closely involved with the creative culture of AfrikaBurn for almost two decades, designing structures that exist in dialogue with the forces around them. Wind, colour, light, and landscape. His latest project, The Crossing, continues this exploration.**
**The team is currently raising funds to complete The Crossing and bring it to Tankwa Town.**
[Join The Crossing campaign](https://www.facebook.com/nathanvhoneysculptures/)
[Follow on the Gram](https://www.instagram.com/nathan.v.honey/)
[Make a donation](https://pos.snapscan.io/qr/MLQXq1bV)
Supporters can contribute to the build through direct donations via SnapScan or EFT, with a selection of project memorabilia offered as a thank-you. 
![](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Richard-Ahlstrom-AfrikaBurn-2025-StvdioɅ-5-1-1024x683.jpg)Artist Nathan V Honey inside Scratch, AfrikaBurn 2025. Photo: Richard Ahlstrom
## With your contribution, The Crossing will continue this journey.
**WHAT THE CROSSING NEEDS  
Every contribution goes directly into building the sculpture.**
Every R1000 donated helps secure one of the following:  
• an 8-ton truck travelling 29 km  
• 109 planks of wood  
• 21.5 litres of enviro-friendly paint  
• 1279 large screws  
• 2778 small screws  
• keeping the crew alive to work another day
**After our initial fundraising, including a generous contribution from AfrikaBurn, we still need to raise R145 370 to fully complete the project.**
**WHAT YOU GET  
All donations are welcome!** In addition to the satisfaction of being part of ‘The Crossing’, and to say thank you for your generous donation, we’ve created a range of unique items connected to ‘The Crossing’ and designed by Nathon V Honey.
R150+ — A limited edition The Crossing Sticker  
R300+ — A sticker and a limited edition fabric patch  
R750+ — A sticker, a patch, and an original red flag, identical to the ones used in ‘The Crossing’  
R2000+ — A sticker, patch, red flag and a photo print from AfrikaBurn 2026, by Jonx Pillemer, signed by the photographer and the artist
All supporters receive a thank-you email with details on how to collect their gift.  
**◇ Donations before 15 April:  
Collect your gift at Arteria during AfrikaBurn.**
◇ Donations after 15 April:  
Collection at AfrikaBurn HQ. If you are not in Cape Town, we can try to arrange delivery.
