Our Annual General Meeting is held in October each year – and this year, it will be held on Saturday October 26th, from 9am to 4pm.

It’s when all Members, Directors and interested community participants review the financial statements and formally adopt them. During the session, we appoint Directors and Members and formally attend to the statutory needs of the AfrikaBurn non-profit company.  
When: Saturday 26th October, 9am – 4pm  
Where: AfrikaBurn HQ, 8 Junction Rd, Salt River, Cape Town  
RSVP: memsec@afrikaburn.com
