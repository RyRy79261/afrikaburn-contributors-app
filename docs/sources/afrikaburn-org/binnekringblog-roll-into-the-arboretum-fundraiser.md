# Roll into the Arboretum Fundraiser

## Step onto the green and into the flow of our Lawn Bowls Bonanza!
**On Sunday, March 31st, gather your team of three at the Mowbray Bowls Club in Cape Town and take aim for a cause at our lawn bowls competition, where your~~roll~~ role contributes towards creating a forest from deforested alien invasives at AfrikaBurn 2024. **
It’s a showdown of strategy and skill, with twists and turns as unpredictable as the winds of the desert. But it’s not just about the bowls – it’s about the buzz, the banter, and the bonds formed over a shared love of art and chainsaws. With food and refreshing beverages to keep you fuelled and refreshed, this is no ordinary day on the green – it’s a gathering of dusty revellers building excitement for the Burn.
**If you aren’t a team player you are welcome to spectate and enjoy the music too. This is a family friendly event.**
