# Streetopia
We’re doing STREETOPIA OBS again! It’s the last Saturday of November (29th), from 10am to 6pm, in the centre of Obs, and you’re invited!
Streetopia is a yearly event hosted in Observatory, as a means to bring some of the delicious AfrikaBurn magic to the city. There’s art, there’s performances, there’s cool music and a host of people doing crazy things. It is again being supported by the City of Cape Town and recognised as a Special Event in their annual calendar.
It’s FREE  
It’s FAMILY FRIENDLY  
It’s FUN
