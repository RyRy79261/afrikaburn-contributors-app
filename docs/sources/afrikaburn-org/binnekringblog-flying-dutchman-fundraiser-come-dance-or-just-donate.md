## The crew of the Flying Dutchman are unfurling her gossamer sails in preparation for a week of spectral sailing across the rock-strewn wastes of the Tankwa Town Binnekring’s deeper reaches.
**To fire our passions for the adventures ahead and to victual our ship, we invite all landlubbers to join us in a freaky fundraiser from 6pm on 28 March at Good Omens in Melville.**
If you can’t make it, you may always support us from afar by unburdening your treasures into our coffers.
Either way, follow this link to Howler to participate in your own special way.
[Get tickets here](https://www.howler.co.za/events/pitch-and-heave-a-flying-dutchman-fundraiser-cf64)
