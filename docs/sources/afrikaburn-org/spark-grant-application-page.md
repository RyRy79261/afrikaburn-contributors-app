AfrikaBurn is offering grants of up to R10 000; to spark a project that advances our guiding principles in the default world.   
Anyone who participated in Out Of The Blue (AfrikaBurn 2025) can apply for a Spark Grant.  
To complete this application, you will need an ID or passport number, cellphone number and email address.

## Criteria:

  * __ Only participants from Out Of The Blue can apply.
  * __ Projects need to take place within the Republic of South Africa.
  * __ Applicants must have an active role in the project applied for.
  * __ All projects are ordinarily to be completed between 1 August 2025 and 31 January 2026.
  * __ Spark Grants can not be used for salaries, fees or stipends.
  * __ Successful applicants will be required to sign a grant agreement and account for their expenditure.
  * __ Previous recipients may not apply for continuation funding.
