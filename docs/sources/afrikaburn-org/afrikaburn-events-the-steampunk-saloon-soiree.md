# The Steampunk Saloon Soiree

Join us on the 28th September for a Steamy Steampunk Saloon Springtime Soiree!

Dust off your finest corsets, top hats, and goggles because we’re taking you on a dusty frolick.

Our fantastic lineup of DJs will keep you grooving all night long:

DJ Martin Luther KWEEN, Jr

Little Wolf

Lady Magnolia

And that’s not all! Brace yourselves for scintillating burlesque performances by our brazen steampunk sirens:

Alchemista, Lola, The Dutchess

Alie/n

Blushette

Kojak Wylder

Lacy Bittz

Lady Magnolia

Magic Elle

Mila Marguerita

Nubia

Purple Rain

Ruby Rouge

Miss Spice C

Vega

Saturday 28th September

Honey & Dora

Doors open at 7pm

Performances start at 8pm so don’t be late!

Tickets R185 – Quicket

But here’s the best part: all the funds raised at this soirée will be channeled into creating the magic of the Steampunk Saloon at the burn in 2024!

So, mark your calendars, assemble your steampunk attire, and get ready for an unforgettable night of revelry, fantasy, and good vibes. We’ll see you at the Steampunk Saloon Soiree!
