## We are in need of sound for our art piece, so we are fundraising in style, with a theatrical social night!
**An evening to get dressed up, to interact and make new friends, to explore an imaginary world and play in a safe and fun environment, to eat, to drink and to be human, or not, depending on how you feel 😉**
What to expect: Interactive theatre, games, puzzles, facilitated conversations, food, drink, performances, vendors to help you get dressed up and more…
The United Worlds Summit is the gathering place for our imagination(s), where the leaders of worlds such as Wonderland, Middle Earth and The Wizarding World converge to collaborate, contemplate and realize what it takes to live harmoniously together.
When not putting your imaginative problem-solving to the test, order a drink from Captain Hook at the Neverland Bar and perhaps figure out the secret ingredient to the infamous Youth Cocktail, chill in the Wonderland tea lounge and have in depth conversations with the Mad Hatter or listen to the most Psychedelic Piano Melodies in the Wonderland chill lounge.
Why not get a tattoo in the symbol of your favourite world, or get our makeup artist to transform you into your favourite world leader.
Don’t forget to get dressed up and represent your favourite world!
There will be a prize for the best dressed =)
