## Ready to Unleash Your Inner Dust Dynamo?  
We’re on the lookout for enthusiastic souls to join us as we embark on a journey of knowledge and prepare to be the heroes of community care!

**What Awaits You?**  
– Dive into mediation techniques that are smoother than a penguin on an ice slide.  
– Master health and safety tips that will make your granny proud.  
– Acquire conflict resolution skills sharper than a juggling flamingo.  
– Learn approaches to contentious situations that are cooler than a cactus in shades.

**What to Bring with?**  
– Snack to share (because sharing is caring, and we’re all about the love!)  
– A drink to stay refreshed (hydration is key, my friend!)  
– Pen & paper (for doodling, note-taking, and creating your Ranger masterpiece)  
– Bring a friend (because the more, the merrier!)

### Date: 24th Feb 2024  
Time: 9 am till 1 pm ish  
Place: AfrikaBurn HQ, 8 Junction Road, Salt River, Cape Town  
Please RSVP to the event and contact Ranger Tigger at [tigger@afrikaburn](mailto:tigger@afrikaburn) for any more info!
