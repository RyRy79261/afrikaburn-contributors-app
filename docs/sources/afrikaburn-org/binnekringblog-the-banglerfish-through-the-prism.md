The Banglerfish surfaced Out Of The Blue at Tankwa Town this year, playing banging Drum and Bass on the Binnenkring. Let’s see what it can do at AfrikaBurn 2026. Just like light refracts to become a rainbow, so The Banglerfish will become something else.

This new creature might not look like a fish at all, more like a cart, with a DJ booth and speakers, able to be pulled by a few people. It might play more styles of breakbeat music, have a better sound system, a bigger glitter ball. Do you have some ideas for a better Banglerfish? If you want to get involved in building, fund raising, operating our little mobile disco at the Burn, or help in any way please get in touch with Princess at princess@ptbb.co.za
