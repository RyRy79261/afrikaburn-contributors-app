## Helloooo fellow burners!

### Last year, we brought this little mobile live stage trailer to AfrikaBurn. The idea was to add an element of acoustic live music to what is often a very electronic playa. We felt it was a huge success, and people really loved it, so this year we want to repeat, improve and expand. This year, we are calling her Sefala Nomadica.

This year we are waiting to include any form of improv performance, from a few songs, to a jam, to a pieces of poetry, a story or anything improvish people are inspired to share.

We are also wanting to improve Sefala Nomadica herself, including, making the platform slightly bigger and stronger, adding a bench, upgrading the sound system slightly, adding some lighting, batteries, an inverter and solar so she is self powered, and although we have run out of time for this year, next year we want to make her self driven by adding electric motors etc. so we are also wanting to get the motors, drive and control gear.

### If you feel inclined to contribute towards this project, please contribute an amount you are inspired to give through our [Back A Buddy campaign here](https://www.backabuddy.co.za/campaign/sefala-nomadica).

Come find us on the Binnekring, we intend to be out most afternoons / sunsets / early evenings as things flow. Come watch and listen – bring your instrument or anything you are inspired to share.

Looking forward to seeing you in the dust!
