Dear fellow adventurers,
Are you prepared to venture into a realm where love, art, and infinite creativity intertwine?
We, a mosaic of artists spanning the world, extend a heartfelt invitation to join us in a wondrous odyssey at Afrikaburn 2024.”
Our Vision:
Our aim is not simply to create art, but to share and spread the message of what we represent, and want to see in the world. “Unity and peace is our collectives driving motto.
Picture this: a vibrant dome rising from the dusty plains of the playa, adorned with intricate designs and pulsating with energy. This is our “Birth giving arena,” a sanctuary where souls unite, creativity flourishes, and the spirit of community thrives. Inspired by the nurturing embrace of a mother’s hands, our structure symbolizes the interconnectedness of all beings and the infinite potential within each of us.
What We Need:
To bring our vision to life, we need your support! Your generous donations will fund the construction of the dome, including the interior design, cladding, and structural elements. Additionally, we’re raising funds for our mesmerizing stage, where the air will come alive with the enchanting melodies of our incredible musicians and DJs. With workshops that ignite the mind and performers that stir the heart.
“Come allow your spirit to be reborn”
