Some heARTists of acclaim you may know, who have had lots & lots & lots of practice, to craft their horns … er, horn their craft, share some titbitz of their learnings and yearnings:

There is nothing more truly artistic, than to fluff people ~ **Vincent Van Gogh**

The object of fluffing … is not to escape reality, but to create a reality of the same intensity ~ **Alberti Giacometti**

Fluffing takes creativity (and effort!) ~ **Henri Matisse**

The job of the artist is always to deepen …the fluffing ~ **Francis Bacon**

To be an artist, is to believe in fluffing ~ **Henry Moore**

You’re only given a little spark of madness. You mustn’t fluff it down! ~ **Robin Williams**

It is not who you fluff that holds you back … it’s who you think you’re not fluffing ~ **Jean-Michel Basquiat**

Let everyone else decide if it is good or bad (technique), whether they looove it or crave more … while they are still deciding, … Keep fluffing! ~ **Andy Warhol**

Fluffing… is for everybody! ~ **Keith Haring**

Fluffing is never finished, only abandoned ~ **Leonardo Da Vinci**

We don’t fluff up mistakes, just happy little accidents. The secret to fluffing… is believing you can do it **~ Bob Ross**

Fluffing is not what you see, but how you make others feel **~ Jackson Pollock**

Fluff… as though your life depends on it, or don’t bother **~ Leigh Bowery**

A fluffer … can reveal things, that other people are terrified of expressing **~ Louise Bourgeois**

If you ask me what I came to do in this world, I, a fluffer, will answer you: I am here to fluff out loud ~ **Émile Zola**

Fluffing is life. Life is fluffing. I never separate it ~ **Ai Weiwei**

Fluffing is contagious! Pass it on! ~ **Albert Einstein**

Those who do not want to fluff anyone, produce nothing ~ **Salvador Dali**

There is no greater agony than bearing an untold fluffing inside you ~ I’ve learned that people will forget what you said, people will forget what you did, but people will never forget how well you fluffed them … ~ **Maya Angelou**

Fluffing has its own emotional embodiment. It carries an emotion with it. When you associate a lyric …, it’s much easier; but when you’re standing there completely dry in front of the camera with no fluffing background, just a fine-tuned, get-this-emotional-story across, it’s a very, very intense kind of focus ~ **Blondie**

Fall in love with yourself, with life and then fluff whoever you want. Everything can have beauty, even the worst fluffer ~ **Frida Kahlo**

The hardest thing is to do something which is close to fluffing, because it is demanding all of you ~ **Marina Abramović**
