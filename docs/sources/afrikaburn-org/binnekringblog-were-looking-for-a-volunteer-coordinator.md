## AfrikaBurn is looking for a full-time Volunteer Coordinator to join our team in Cape Town until 30 September 2026.

**We’re looking for an organised, people-focused professional who thrives in community environments and understands that strong volunteer coordination is the backbone of large-scale collaborative projects. This role supports and strengthens AfrikaBurn’s volunteer ecosystem across portfolios and the annual event cycle.**

AfrikaBurn is the strategic driver of Burning Man culture in the Southern African region and plays a significant role in the arts and culture sector of South Africa. Most known for its flagship event in the Tankwa Karoo, as a public benefit organisation, AfrikaBurn is the human scaffold that helps a diverse creative community bring numerous projects and experiences to life.

**Key responsibilities**

  * **Volunteer Systems & Administration:****  
**➢ Manage volunteer databases, registrations, placements, and reporting;  
➢ Maintain accurate records and improve volunteer processes and workflows;  
➢ Support day-to-day administrative functions of the volunteer portfolio.
  * **Volunteer Coordination & Support:****  
**➢ Coordinate recruitment, onboarding, and placement across portfolios;  
➢ Act as liaison between volunteers, portfolio leads, and event structures;  
➢ Support scheduling, communication, conflict resolution, and retention initiatives;  
➢ Foster a volunteer culture aligned with AfrikaBurn’s ethos.
  * **Communications & Operations:****  
**➢ Manage volunteer communications and ensure timely, accurate information flow;  
➢ Collaborate internally to align volunteer capacity with operational needs;  
➢ Contribute to reporting and flag risks relating to volunteer welfare or culture;  
➢ Support volunteer coordination before, during, and after the annual event.
  * **Event Coordination and Community Campaigns:****  
**➢ Event planning and event coordination;  
➢ Content creation, scheduling, and designing platforms for engagement;  
➢Apply design thinking towards community intervention ;  
➢ Show creative skills in product design and networking.



**This position does require travel to Tankwa and occasional weekend/evening work.**

**Minimum Requirements**  

  * A diploma or degree in Community Development, Event Management, Public Relations or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  * Proven experience coordinating volunteers, community projects, or large-scale events.
  * Strong organisational, administrative, and communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple stakeholders effectively.
  * Is familiar with social media platforms and confident in content creation 
  * Proficiency in Google Workspace (especially Sheets and Docs) and comfort working with database systems and digital tools.
  * Ability to learn and work within an hybrid environment
  * Has own car and drivers
  * Able to work in Cape Town



**Traits (We value these highly)  **

  * Collaborative and enthusiastic to work with others
  * Proactive about problem-solving and creating solutions
  * Self motivated with good work ethic and time management skills
  * Attention to detail - methodical, efficient, and meticulous
  * Dynamic and able to work in a changing environment
  * Conscientious; a strong sense of confidentiality, ethics, duty and personal responsibility
  * Respectful of collective decision-making and consultative processes 
  * Excellent interpersonal skills, able to work with people at all levels
  * Able to embrace the unknown and thrive in a pressured environment 



**To apply for this role, please submit a CV and covering letter by 13 March 2026 cob to[ jobs@afrikaburn.com](mailto:jobs@afrikaburn.com) with the subject line: Volunteer Coordinator.**
