## In 2024, The Motherfucking Queens are set to make the Binnekring sparkle once again, inviting all curious souls to visit our oasis of divine feminine energy. Come flow, vibe, sing, dance and witness the inaugural voyage of our new mutant vehicle. This year we celebrate inclusivity, unity and we pay homage to our host nation South Africa.
**Empowerment and Divine Celebration**  
At The Queens, we channel the divine feminine, celebrate beauty in its myriad forms, and kindle the royal flame within each of us. Our camp serves as a sanctuary and a vibrant altar for all who seek to commune with their inner sovereignty. Prepare to be transformed through workshops and experiences that will ignite your inner Motherfucking Queen!
**Workshops and Spaces of Inspiration**  
Recall the diverse workshops that sparked inspiration last year? Did someone say Tantra, Shibari or maybe the infamous Latino Night? We’re elevating this experience with an even more robust schedule designed to tap into your majestic essence. Our spaces are being thoughtfully curated to make you feel cherished and uplifted, from a desert oasis living room to a serene cuddle puddle zone, all infused with our signature scent – come smell us!
**Evening Rhythms and Thematic Days**  
Our communal space will come alive with a curated music program each evening offering more than the usual dunz dunz dunz. Workshops from Monday to Saturday will unfold around daily themes, designed to offer unique and unforgettable experiences:
**Monday: Adventures and Discovery**  
**Tuesday: Communication**  
**Wednesday: Body and Mind**  
**Thursday: Sensuality and Connection**  
**Friday: Culture and Connection**  
**Saturday: Creativity and Art**
## **Special Events to Mark**
**Queen Makeda’s Sunset: the Beats of Sheba:** A musical celebration of the bold and beautiful women who helped shape the African continent. It will be hosted on our zebra art car (Punda Milia) somewhere on the Binnekring.
**Wild Wedding:** We joyfully invites couples, throuples, fruples and soccer teams of any size or identity to celebrate their union with a majestic Queens’ wedding ceremony. Embracing the wild beauty of love, ceremonies will be guided with an untamed animalistic theme, come dance, twirl and roar to celebrate your union under the African sun.
**We’re pouring our hearts into crafting an unforgettable experience for our second year on the Binnekring. The magic we co-create with you, our fellow Burners, is what truly brings our vision to life. Join us for another year of transformation, connection, and celebration at The Motherfucking Queens… and until then, stay royal!**
