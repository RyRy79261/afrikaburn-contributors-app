## Spark Grants 2019 – Applications Open!
  * [ __ TravisLyle  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/travislyle/)
  * [ __ May 29, 2019 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2019/05/29/)
  * __ 2:36 pm

  * __ Categories: [Latest News](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/latest-news/)

Our Spark Grants are now in their 3rd year – and applications for the 2019 cycle are now open!  
**What are Spark Grants?**  
Designed to kickstart projects that positively benefit communities across South Africa, the grants exist to bring the principles that inform AfrikaBurn alive in some way. Over the past 2 years, the grants have seen a very wide variety of community initiatives and social development projects funded, including community & school food gardens, environmental campaigns, community bicycle workshops, theatre and arts workshops and many others.  
**How do they work?**  
Funding of R5000 is provided for each grant, to spark a project that advances our guiding principles in the default world. Anyone can apply for a Spark Grant, but the project needs to be nominated (in addition to the applicant) by a participant of Ephemeropolis (AfrikaBurn 2019).  
You will need the ID or passport number; cell phone number; and email address of a nominator.  
General criteria:
  * Projects need to take place within the Republic of South Africa
  * All projects are ordinarily to be completed between 1 August 2019 and 31 January 2020
  * Spark Grants can not be used for salaries or stipends.
  * Successful applicants will be required to sign a grant agreement and account for their expenditure.
  * Previous applicants may not apply for the same project.

  
Application deadline: 30 June 2019 12h00 (GMT+2).  
To view previous Spark Grant projects, [click on over to this page](https://www.afrikaburn.com/community/afrikaburn-year-round/outreach) – and to apply to be awarded a Grant, [head to this page and use the form](https://tribe.afrikaburn.com/form/spark-grant-2019), or [download a copy](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/SPARK-GRANTS-Application-Form-2019.docx) and send back to our team for the application to be considered.
