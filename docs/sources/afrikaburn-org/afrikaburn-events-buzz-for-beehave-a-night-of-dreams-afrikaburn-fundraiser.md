# Buzz for BeeHave – A night of dreams (AfrikaBurn Fundraiser)

Join us for a night of fun and excitement at the **Galerie Hoang Beli**! Let’s come together to support BeeHave’s journey to AfrikaBurn. This special event is set to take place on February 15th. It’s the perfect opportunity to connect with others, enjoy delicious food, and dance the night away. Here’s what you can look forward to:

**Event Highlights**

- Crepe party served by our camp’s finest members
- Bee-themed cocktails that will keep you buzzing through the evening
- Live DJs: Hand-picked Parisian talent that will bring the spirit of the Binnekring to the Marais

**Why Attend?**

This event is more than just a fundraiser; it’s a chance to be part of a vibrant community. AfrikaBurn is known for its creativity and inclusivity, and this event promises to embody those values. Whether you’re a longtime supporter or new to the AfrikaBurn community, everyone is welcome to mingle and make new friends.

Don’t miss out on this fantastic evening in Paris! Mark your calendars and come ready to have a great time. We can’t wait to see you there!
