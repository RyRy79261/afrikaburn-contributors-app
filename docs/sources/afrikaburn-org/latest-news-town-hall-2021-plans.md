## Town Hall: 2021 Plans
  * [ __ TravisLyle  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/travislyle/)
  * [ __ October 29, 2020 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2020/10/29/)
  * __ 9:50 am

  * __ Categories: [Latest News](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/latest-news/)

It’s a whole new world – and in it, we’re all finding new ways of being and doing. And importantly as we look to the future, we’re looking toward Quaggafontein.  
[](https://www.facebook.com/events/359044238717310/)  
  
How is Quaggafontein different or similar to the location of our previous desert gatherings, Stonehenge? To answer this question, over the last year members of the AfrikaBurn team, along with specialists in a variety of fields (including soil geologists, archaeologists and heritage consultants) have spent time on the land, analysing and surveying the landscape to determine what the appropriate next steps could be, and what areas would be suitable for hosting gatherings in the desert.  
As we find our feet in this new space, so we’re inviting you to this Town Hall to ask questions, and for our team to get a sense of what you, our community members, see when you look into the future. What are your fears, hopes, dreams, apprehensions and bright ideas? Let’s hear them, and talk about them.  
Got questions or suggestions? Join us at the Town Hall – let’s map the future together!  
This is an online discussion and on the night, members of our AfrikaBurn Operations team will be on hand to field questions and respond to your suggestions or queries on the meeting link provided.  
REGISTER  
To join the Town Hall, you’d need to register – [and you can do that here.](https://us02web.zoom.us/…/register/WN_X8fXzPQFSCSOYP7ttOpMkA)  
We look forward to having you join the conversation!
