**The latest AfrikaBurn Town Hall meeting was held on 25 October at 7:00pm (SAST).** **Click the video link below and you’ll be able to watch the AfrikaBurn Mission Control team discuss Die Hek (The Gate) and chat all things ticketing and getting ready with project registrations for AfrikaBurn 2023.**

Hosted by members of our Client Liaison team, including Julia Savage who’s the manager of all things [tickets](http://tickets.afrikaburn.com), Box Office and Gate. Julia has the answers to life, the universe and all of the questions that you might have about getting your hands on tickets and getting off the launchpad to Space at AfrikaBurn 2023. We’ve done away with DDT’s this year (we’ll explain why at the Town Hall) and New Horizon Tickets have almost sold out, so if you don’t have a ticket and plan to buy one in the General Sale on 2 November 2022, this is an important mission debriefing for you.
