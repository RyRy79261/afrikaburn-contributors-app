## Beloved Burners! We are recruiting for the Naked Spectrum Art-Walk 2026! As a celebration of our human form, we paint ourselves into a naked colourful parade that will take place on the Friday of the burn at 12 noon at Birthday Suits*. 

**This year, we are limiting numbers to around 100, and participants will be able to have a shower again after the walk to wash the paint off.**

Community empowered Radical Self-Expression donning one very fine outfit: you and your fave colour! We would like to extend an early invitation to participate to our previous attendees.

If you are keen to join for the first time, or would like to participate again this year, please email me your colour of choice and a backup colour. Colour choices are: Green, Blue, Purple, Pink and Orange. We do ask for a contribution please of R265 towards your paint, application sponge, flow bin flocculent for the paint wash off and other equipment we may need etc. Please confirm attendance and make payment at your earliest convenience or by April 7th.

**If you would like to book your spot, please EFT R265 with your name and colour as a reference to:**  
ABSA cheque account: Matthew Liebenberg  
Acc number : 4091652004  
Branch code: 632005

For those based overseas, you are welcome to PayPal me on @mattliebe. Please pay an additional R40 for exchange fees etc.

Lastly, we would greatly appreciate help from volunteers to assist us with distributing paints etc. We are also asking anyone with a bakkie who is willing to please bring us the flow bin for the paint wash-off from the Hek to Birthday Suits and back again. If anyone is available to help us on the day, please let me know.

**We can’t wait to get colourful with you!**

**Matthew +27 73 133 8949**  
**email:**[**nakedspectrum@gmail.com**](mailto:nakedspectrum@gmail.com)

*If the weather is poor, we will move it to noon on Saturday.
