**Frankie Smalls writes:** “Dear Afrikaburn community, our beloved friend, father, son, husband, long time DPW member Sammy Minde has been tragically killed and his family needs our help. I have started this campaign to raise funds to cover the costs of his funeral and the costs for a brick wall to be built around his house … Sammy’s murderers have been set free back into his neighbourhood, and his wife and children are worried for their safety.

**Sammy once told me how he felt safe for the first time in his life at Afrikaburn.** His time spent in DPW meant the world to him. He was often seen operating Afrikaburn’s forklift, maybe he has helped you move something at Burn.”

**Sammy touched everyone who’s ever spent time in Tankwa Town by being a part of the DPW Family. Frankie has created a[Backabuddy](https://www.backabuddy.co.za/champion/project/frankie-smalls-5672019571312041705) page for a campaign to help. It’s off to a flying start, but we’d love to do more and help provide as much support as we can for his family. No contribution is too small.**
