# Community Project Directory
Lost? You are here: [Home](https://www.afrikaburn.org/) > [Event](https://www.afrikaburn.org/event/) > [Participation](https://www.afrikaburn.org/event/participation/) > Community Project Directory
**A Community Directory? Really? Finally?**
Yes. Now you can register your project, and get people to be involved! 
And for those looking for projects to get involved with? That’s also here. It’s all working, it’s all exciting, and we get to do it now!
### Looking for people to join your creative project?
Head on down the page, and fill out the registration form for new Projects. Once it’s filled in, your application will be reviewed, and we’ll load it up on the directory if it’s a valid, registered project. 
### Do you want to join a creative project looking for participants?
Just below, there’s a directory of all the available projects in our Community Project Directory. You can browse them, and use the form on each listing to contact their contact person. 
If you’re struggling in any way, or have any feedback or suggestions (this is a totally new thing) please feel free to email [info@afrikaburn.com](mailto:info@afrikaburn.com).

**Needed:** 🌟 Who We’re Looking For – The Fractal Chill Crew Fractal Chill is a working, gifting-based camp, and we welcome humans who embody contribution, kindness, reliability, and a love for community. We’re looking for people who feel naturally inspired to help create a Bedouin wonderland: tea-makers, coffee lovers, musos, creatives, builders, homestead helpers, and anyone who enjoys being part of a team effort. Ideal crew members are self-motivated, easy-going, and willing to gift at least two hours a day in the Café, Muso, Arty, Builder, or Homestead teams — plus one hour supporting camp hygiene, kitchen care, showers, or MOOP. We value people who show up, participate fully, help during build and strike, and see the camp as a shared home. If you love ceremony, comfort, teamwork, and co-creating magic in the dust, you’ll fit right in with the Fractal Chill family.
