## We are Moon Village, and we are on a mission to create a celestial lounge at Afrikaburn.
**Picture a space where connection, relaxation, and creativity intertwine to form the ultimate sanctuary.** From consensual cuddle puddles and sound baths to chai tea gatherings and immersive art sessions, there’s something for everyone at our theme camp. But we can’t do it alone – we need your support to make Moon Village come to life!
**We will be announcing a few fun fundraising parties in Cape Town on our instagram page soon, follow us:<https://www.instagram.com/zemoonvillage?igsh=eGY0dnFpam5qYjRu>**
So, dust off your binnekring attire, gather your tribe, and join us for a fundraising celebration. Together, let’s make Moon Village a reality and elevate the experience of all who journey to AfrikaBurn 2024.
