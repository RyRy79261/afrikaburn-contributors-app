## Need info? It’s here

  * [ __ Other Author  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/otherauthor/)
  * [ __ April 23, 2012 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2012/04/23/)
  * __ 1:26 pm

  * __ Categories: [Binnekring Blog](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/binnekringblog/)

OK, alright, good, great, excellent, marvellous, fantastic! IT’S FINALLY TIME! If you’re reading this, you’re probably attending **AfrikaBurn 2012** and need info.  
Right, first things first:

### HAVE YOU GOT YOUR TICKET?

You won’t be allowed in without one. Bring Photo ID too.  
Kids 14 and under – no ticket required – but do bring a Photo ID as proof of age.  
**TICKET NAME CHANGES ARE CLOSED – BUT IT’S OK!**  
This message is from Quicket, AfrikaBurn’s ticket vendor:  
We can no longer change ticket holder names and re-issue tickets. It is not the end of the world though. If you are giving your ticket to someone please make sure of the following:  
• They MUST bring the ticket to the event, no ticket means no access as we cannot verify them against our database.  
• Each ticket can only be used once, make sure that the original ticket holder is NOT coming to the event, we do not want to have to turn people away.  
If the above is followed then there should not be a problem.

### DIRECTIONS

http://www.afrikaburn.com/the-event/getting-there

### BRINGING ART THAT HASN’T BEEN REGISTERED?

No problem – arrive, check in at Off-Centre Camp and ask for the Art Crew.

### MEDIA – FILM CREWS – PHOTOGRAPHERS – JOURNOS

When you arrive, you’ll need to be tagged and released. Please get to Off-Centre Camp and check in at the Media Booth.

### WANNA VOLUNTEER?

Be sure to come along to the Volunteer Booth at Off-Centre Camp (corner of 6ish street and Binnekring road) We want you we need you and AfrikaBurn would not happen without you!!

### ALL OTHER INFO

This website has it – nose around and sniff it out.  
Fingers, toes, nose and shoes. You’re good to go. Hit the road!
