# Ain’t Misbehavin’ – a fundraiser at The Electric

Ain’t Misbehavin’ is back with another party, to bring The World’s Finest Dusty Ballroom back to AfrikaBurn…

Step into a world of vintage allure and dusty glamour. Dress in your vintage-burn-best or let your creativity run wild—this is your night to make a statement.

A magical evening awaits, featuring:  
– Live Swing Jazz & Blues with the talented Lilavan & Friends  
– a Blues Dance Intro Workshop to get you grooving  
– Tantalising Shadow Burlesque by Ruby Rouge and Nubia  
– Timeless tunes with DJs Matt Willis and Lucky Mezzcal

WHEN: Saturday 12 April 2025, 7 PM – 2 AM  
WHERE: The Electric (48 Canterbury St, District 6)

TICKETS:  
– First 20 tickets: R150  
– Early-bird (until 12 March): R180  
– Regular tickets: R260  
– At the door: R300

Book now, and revel in a night of dusty vintage allure. Tickets are limited!

If you can’t make it to our party, you can still support our fundraiser, or sponsor a dancer to teach & perform at AfrikaBurn 🙂 Donation options in the ticket link….
