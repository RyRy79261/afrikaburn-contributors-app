**🌊🚍 TAKE A BUS TO AFRIKABURN 2025 – OUT OF THE BLUE & INTO THE DUST! 🔥**  
For over 8 years, we’ve been helping Burners get to AfrikaBurn safely, easily, and with good vibes. Since 2024, this journey has also been a FUNdraiser for Camp Enos Nookie, and we’re proud to support them again this year!
By riding with us, you’re helping Camp Enos Nookie bring more art, music, and cheeky antics to the Burn. Let’s make sure they deliver a Galactic Plasma Pulse and Quantum Funk Blast to the dust!
🗓**2025 Bus Dates**
DEPARTURE  
Cape Town → AfrikaBurn
Mon, April 28 – 1 AM  
Wed, April 30 – 1 AM  
Fri, May 2 – 1 AM
RETURN  
AfrikaBurn → Cape Town  
Wed, April 30 – 8 AM  
Fri, May 2 – 8 AM  
Sun, May 4 – 1:30 PM  
Need a different date? Let us know, and we’ll try to make it happen!
Why Choose Take A Bus?  
Support Camp Enos Nookie – Your ticket helps this camp bring extra Interstellar Nookie Surge energy to the Burn!  
Cheaper than renting a car – More cash for your Burn experience!  
No flat tires or dust headaches – Our buses are Tankwa-tested and Burner-approved!  
The Burn starts on the bus! – Good tunes, great people, and endless Burner spirit!
🔥🐵🌈 The Nookie Love Boost! 🌈🐵🔥  
Can’t make it but still want to support Camp Enos Nookie? The Nookie Love Boost lets you add some ✨ magic ✨ when booking your bus or anytime you’re feeling generous!
Your donation helps bring more 🎶 beats, 🍹 treats, and 🙈 cheeky antics to AfrikaBurn. It’s like giving Nookie a Mondo Nebula Wave of energy!
🌈 No donation is too small! Whether it’s a Tiny Stardust or an Elon Musk’s Galactic Mind-Fuck, your support keeps the Burn spirit alive!
💸 Add a donation when booking or anytime you want to spread the love!
🎭💖🐒 BIG LOVE & BANANAS! 🐒💖🎭
🚌 Ready to Ride?  
Get all the details and book your spot here: <https://www.bustoafrikaburn.com>
Join the Take A Bus family and support Camp Enos Nookie—let’s make this Burn unforgettable!
