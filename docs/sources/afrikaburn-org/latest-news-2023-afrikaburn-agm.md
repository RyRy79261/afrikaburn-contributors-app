**AfrikaBurn 's Annual General Meeting** will take place on 28 October. It's an important milestone where our Non-Profit Organisation performs a significant amount of governance as well as structural and financial obligations. It is crucially attended by our Members and Directors, but anyone can attend if they want to, and anyone can add to the agenda too.

**If you are interested in participating, join us here<https://youtube.com/live/eAYBj8lrLhg> on 28 October at 10 am SAST. Contact [info@afrikaburn.com](mailto:info@afrikaburn.com) with agenda additions or questions. **
