## If you’ve earned your sea-legs on board the good ship AfrikaBurn, if you know how to weather a storm and survive the doldrums, if you can juggle more balls than an octopus, and you know that time and tide waits for no (hu)man … then perhaps its time to chart your course for a seat on the AfrikaBurn board?
**Non-Executive Directors (NEDs) help to steer the strategic ship of Afrika Burns Creative Projects (or “the organisation”) and play an important role in ensuring that the ethics and governance are maintained, actioned and implemented, to safeguard our community.**
**Candidates will be asked to commit to giving at least 20 hours per month of their time in service to the organisation that propels the movement we know as AfrikaBurn.**
Afrika Burns Creative Projects is a registered non-profit company and public benefit organisation with section 18A status. With a commitment to our cultural context, AfrikaBurn is driven to ensure that our decision-making circles at all levels are inclusive and represent the diversity of South Africa. 
#### We specifically encourage women of colour to apply, and the AfrikaBurn Membership has committed to holding one available seat. If this is you, we’d love you to consider applying.
#### We have two NED spaces available and we’re scanning the horizon for individuals with experience and expertise in the following areas:
  * Cultural Activism
  * Environmental & Land Management
  * Finance 
  * Fundraising
  * Information Technology
  * Law

#### With the following soft skills and attributes:
  * Previous South African non-profit board experience essential
  * Independence
  * Strong ethics
  * Willingness to challenge
  * Coaching ability
  * Be self-reflective
  * Curiosity
  * Patience
  * Capability
  * An understanding of South Africa’s cultural environment and/ or pan African view
  * Willing and able to proactively engage with compliance requirements of being a NED

## **The deadline for applications is 15 September.**
