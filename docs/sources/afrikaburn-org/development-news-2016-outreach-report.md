# 2016 Outreach Report

We are pleased to present the [**AfrikaBurn 2016 Outreach Report**](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Outreach-FY16.pdf). This document provides a detailed account of our efforts to engage with and support the communities surrounding the Tankwa Karoo region over the past year. Our initiatives focused on art collaborations, educational programs, and environmental sustainability projects, all aimed at extending the values of AfrikaBurn beyond the event itself. We have worked closely with local partners to foster community development and meaningful connections. We invite you to review our activities and achievements outlined in this report and thank you for your continued support.
