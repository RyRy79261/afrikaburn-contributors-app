# Meet Our Star Volunteer: Bobo-Ashanti

Shout out for your never-ending joy. For being one half of the Cutest Duo. For capturing DPW at their best (because you bring out the best in them). You hit DPW hard; the resonance will be felt long after you leave. You were the delight in the desert and in the trying times – you brought the light.
**Name/Burner Name:** My name is Sinethemba Mzinyathi, burner name BOBO-ASHANTI
**For how long have you been part of AfrikaBurn?** This was my second year attending although burn has been part of my orbit, even on my first time it felt more home
**What made you want to join the community?** After attending my first burn I was more thrilled and eager to get more involved. Every shift I volunteered on broadened my insight, the benefiting part about volunteering you build a connection with the community.
**What is your role within the community?** DEE PEE DOUBLE YOU (DPW) SHADE-CREW! But I believe I’m more than that. I help anyhow and wherever I’m needed, being part of DPW expands on radical self-inclusion and communal effort.
**What do you love about AfrikaBurn?** I’ve been searching for words and feelings to describe my experience. This year I was more in a witnessing position (mostly) the backscene with DPW to see how magic connects. I’m mostly engaged by gratitude every time I think about the burn, it’s anthological to put on words. Mostly grateful with the set of skills I’ve departed with from the desert – no one ever speaks about the moment you step unto the PLAYA maybe that day you’re feeling a bit overwhelmed or a bit sad it’s as though that the people can feel/sense it – they give you hugs that would set your frequency right. I miss desert hugs.
**Fun facts!** I I’m a lense based artist but for the first time I wasn’t as close to my camera as I usually would be , I didn’t take more than 100pictures , every time I looked through the viewfinder I couldn’t see due to the fact my eye were filled with tears , for the first time I wept more than once not because of sad situation because of thousands of strangers united by love.  
We appreciate you, BOBO, and are thrilled to have you on our #WALL OF FIRE!
