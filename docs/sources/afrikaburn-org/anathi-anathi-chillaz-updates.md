**Hola! Heita! Hi- Chillaz 2023, What’s next?**
Chillaz 2023 promises an immersive African experience with participants bringing –
drumming to the fore, afro house bands, South African Jazz, Zimbabwean Mbira circles, Adinkra Inspired body painting, our resident DJ’s matched with flowing African Dance move lessons and late-night incense infused poetry and screening session.
We thank all participants who have applied through the Anathi Tickets & Access Grants programme.
We also especially thank the many Burners who have been inspired by Chillaz and have reached out to collaborate, support and participate in our exciting upcoming Chillaz space programme. Be sure to purchase yours or your crew’s allocated tickets by the 28th of February 2023.
**One Burner One Shift!**  
[Signup to volunteer at Chillaz .](https://www.afrikaburn.org/event/participation/) We promise great music and afro flavour snacks!
Lookout for our various Volunteer Days and our upcoming Anathi Fundraising Event.
