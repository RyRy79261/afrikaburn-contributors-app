**Report from Quaggafontein event site:**  

It is with deep sadness that we report that on Thursday, May 1st, a 44 year old Swiss male participant passed away after collapsing on site at AfrikaBurn.
They were walking across the event site with a friend in the early evening when they unexpectedly collapsed to the ground. Nearby participants rapidly assisted and rushed them to our nearby on-site medical facility, where further diagnosis and attempts at resuscitation were made by the AfrikaBurn professional medical teams. Unfortunately, resuscitation was unsuccessful and the participant passed away on site.
AfrikaBurn would like to thank the participants who assisted on the scene, the AfrikaBurn medical team, Medics in Motion, Doctors in Motion, Medextreme and the South African Police Service for their prompt attention and care.
If anyone at the event witnessed the incident and would like access to confidential, specialist emotional support from our team, please contact [support@afrikaburn.com](mailto:support@afrikaburn.com)
We expect further updates to follow, as next of kin are currently being notified. For any clarification on this statement, please contact [directors@afrikaburn.com](mailto:directors@afrikaburn.com)
