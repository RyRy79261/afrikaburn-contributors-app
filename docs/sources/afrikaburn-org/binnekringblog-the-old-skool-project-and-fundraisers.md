## Somewhere out in the Tankwa dust stands an old schoolhouse. This is not a mirage, but a vision moving through a prism into reality as The Old Skool. The vision is one of dance and play, where 70s, 80s, 90s and 00s anthems echo down the skool corridors, calling us back to simpler times and to the soundtrack of our youth.
**The Old Skool is hosting three opportunities to support this project:**
**Joburg fundraiser:** The Old Skool Fete  
Date: Saturday, 7 March from 2pm  
Venue: Independent Traders, Northriding  
Tickets available on Quicket: <https://www.quicket.co.za/events/311415-the-old-skool-fete/#/>
**Online Pub Quiz**  
Date: Sunday 29 March, 6pm BST / 7pm SAST  
Venue: Zoom meeting  
Sign up on BackABuddy: <https://www.backabuddy.co.za/campaign/old-skool-fundraiser-quiz>
If you can’t make it to either event but would still like to make a donation, please do so here: <https://www.backabuddy.co.za/campaign/the-old-skool-fundraiser>
