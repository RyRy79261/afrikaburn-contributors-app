OPEN CALL TO ARTISTS ✨  
Calling sticker artists & paste-up creatives!  
Be part of The Sticker Magnet, a 2-meter-tall rectangular column art installation rising from the Tankwa Karoo desert at AfrikaBurn 2026. With four faces and endless character, it’s a one-of-a-kind gallery for your non-commercial stickers and paste-ups.  
How to participate:  
🧍 In person  
Find The Sticker Magnet in The Binnekring at AfrikaBurn 2026 and add your work yourself. Paste will be provided.  
📬 Remotely  
• Paste-ups: Email digital files to dawie.venter@gmail.com  
• Stickers: Mail physical stickers to:  
40 Dianthus Street, Heldervue, Somerset West, 7130, South Africa  
(Mail early—local post can be unreliable)  
I’ll print and paste a limited number of submitted works on your behalf.  
Guidelines:  
• Non-commercial work only  
• Max A4 size, up to 4 designs per artist  
• Limited space & funds apply  
🗓 Deadline: 11 March 2026  
📸 Progress shared at @DawieUrbanArt  
Let your art stick in the desert 🌵
