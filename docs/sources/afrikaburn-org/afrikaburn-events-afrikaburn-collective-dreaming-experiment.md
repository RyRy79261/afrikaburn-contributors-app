# AfrikaBurn Collective Dreaming Experiment
**Are you missing Afrikaburn?** We are too! Join us in this online gathering to connect with the AfrikaBurn Community from around the world, share memories from your last Burn and to experiment with collective dreaming.
**  
Bring a memento or photo from the last Burn you attended to share with the group.** We’ll start by connecting and sharing.
  
Next, we’ll have a guided meditation and visualization to lead us into a process of collective dreaming where we’ll share our hopes and dreams for the world and how we bring the AfrikaBurn principles into our lives.
  
When: Aug 3, 2024 10:00 AM Johannesburg
