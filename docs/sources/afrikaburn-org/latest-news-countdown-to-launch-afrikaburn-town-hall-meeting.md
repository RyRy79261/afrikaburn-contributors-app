**It’s less than 100 days until we burn the Clan, and it’s higher-grade planning and preparation time for those of you coming to join us in SPACE this year.** To help you with your launch plans, the next town hall meeting will be held on the 1st of February at 6 pm SAST. We’ll have representatives from the Theme Camps, Mutant Vehicles and our Suppliers teams on hand to answer any questions that you might have as you begin your countdown.
You might be wondering if you should still register a theme camp? How best to join someone else’s camp or even create your own? What to do if you need a large bedouin tent or have already arranged delivery of a luxury glamping space pod with its own galley of slaves? What makes a mutant a mutant? Why electric scooters are good in the city but awkward in the desert? Why everyone is encouraged to explore [SPACE](https://tickets.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-2023-theme/), but not to stray too far from the Quaggafontein mothership and end up on another planet? Should you buy that fake vehicle pass on Viagogo? **(Quick answer … NO!)**.
**All are welcome to join us via Zoom:** <https://us06web.zoom.us/j/88263141518> (don’t click till the meeting starts)
**If you have any specific questions** you would like to be answered in this upcoming town hall then please email them directly to [brain@afrikaburn.com](mailto:brain@afrikaburn.com)
