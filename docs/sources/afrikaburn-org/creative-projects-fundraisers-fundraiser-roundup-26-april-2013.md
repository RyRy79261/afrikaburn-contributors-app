## 
## THE LOVE MACHINE’S LAST RAISER
Please join us on the 27th of April for one final little shake and boogie before the burn that is AfrikaBurn. Come have some fun and help us raise some funds for one of the most exciting projects to hit Tankwa Town.
Whether you are coming to the Burn or not we have a devastating lineup up of DJ’s from far and near :  
Atish (USA) – <https://soundcloud.com/atish>  
Mark Slee (USA) – <https://soundcloud.com/mcslee>  
Diggin4Dodge  
Agugio
Atish and Mark Slee are in SA for their first AfrikaBurn experience. These seasoned Burning Man vets are gonna come see how we do it on African soil.  
If you are coming to Tankwa come get a preview. If you are not, this will be the only change to catch them.  
@Fiction 226 Long Street  
Tickets available at the door:  
R50 – All night  
Info: [https://www.facebook.com/events/149655975208264/ ](https://www.facebook.com/events/149655975208264/)

## PLANETARY BINDER – LAST RAISER
The Planetary Binder team, have a request to all DJs who are in Cape Town this Saturday: they’re hosting another last minute fundraiser – and it’s it’s a warehouse party in a secret, never seen before, venue. They have an international act, as well as two bands performing and are looking for more acts to pull in and join the vibe.
The party starts at 12:00 midday, for a day time chill with some beers and chilled beats and then turns into a fully blown party at night. Whether you will be able to join or not, they’d would like to request that you share the vibe with your peeps on Facebook.
Please just copy paste the following: Underground warehouse party this weekend.  
“Join the party” on: [www.planetary-binder.com](http://www.planetary-binder.com) … Venue details will be sent to you 2hrs before the party starts. Day Vibe, Night Jam. Share what you love, bring your band, showcase your art, display your invention. For those you love and those who love.  
[facebook.com/pages/Planetary-Binder/266035580197221](http://facebook.com/pages/Planetary-Binder/266035580197221)
