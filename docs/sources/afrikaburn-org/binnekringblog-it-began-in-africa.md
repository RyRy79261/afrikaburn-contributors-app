# It Began in Africa

(text and photos: Ali B)
  
Alongside being a regular at Burning Man in the US for the last six years, 2013 will be my fourth year in a row attending AfrikaBurn. I always look forward to DJing out there and it’s been a real privilege being part of the [LedHedz](http://www.facebook.com/ledhedz "LedHedz on Facebook") crew.  
I somehow always seem to land a prime spot DJing on the night of the burn, whether its on the VuVu Lounge or any of the other sound systems. I also look forward to playing on Sunday night – last year I think I played for nearly nine hours from 9pm – 6am which was a lot of fun. Here are some of the films I have made over the years alongside the DJ mix I put together for last year, enjoy!
