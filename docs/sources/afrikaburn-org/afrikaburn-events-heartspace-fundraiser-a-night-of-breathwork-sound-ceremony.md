# ✨ Heartspace Fundraiser: A Night of Breathwork, Sound & Ceremony ✨
Join us for a deeply immersive evening of healing, transformation, and connection, guided by holistic practitioner Clare. This special fundraiser for Heartspace will take you on a journey through breath, sound, and sacred ceremony—offering clarity, release, and deep peace.
🔥 What to Expect:  
✨ Ecstatic Breathwork – Circular breathing for transcendental states & breakthroughs  
✨ Yoga Nidra & Sound Journey – Live vocal looping, poetry & sacred soundscapes  
✨ Candlelight Intention Setting – Reflect, center & reconnect  
✨ Sacred Ceremony – Aromatherapy, Reiki, gongs, crystal bowls, drumming & mantras
💫 What to Bring:  
🧘 Yoga mat  
🛌 Blanket & pillow (plus extra for back support if needed)  
💧 Bottle of water  
🕯️ A candle & a small item for the altar  
😌 Eye mask (or something to cover your eyes)  
📖 A journal (optional) for before / after event  
❤️ Openness & curiosity
📌 Important: Eat a full meal 4 hours before, and only something light 2 hours before to avoid discomfort during breathwork.
✨ Whether you join to participate fully or simply absorb the energy, this evening promises to be soul-nourishing. We can’t wait to share this sacred space with you! 💛
