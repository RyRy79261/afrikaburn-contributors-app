## 2020 Tickets On Hold

  * [ __ TravisLyle  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/travislyle/)
  * [ __ March 12, 2020 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2020/03/12/)
  * __ 11:53 am

  * __ Categories: [Latest News](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/latest-news/)

In consideration of the uncertain status of large gatherings taking place in South Africa due to the effects of the coronavirus, we have paused ticket sales as of 9am this morning, March 12th. To all of our community members that are presently involved in preparing projects and plans, we absolutely understand that this will come as a concern and request that you await our next bulletin to get a sense of what the next steps will be.  
We’re currently in discussion with the relevant authorities and are awaiting feedback from them, and really appreciate your understanding during this unpredictable time.
