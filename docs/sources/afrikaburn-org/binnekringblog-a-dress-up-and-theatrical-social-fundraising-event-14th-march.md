## The 8th Day of Creation is hosting a fundraiser in tandem with its [crowdfunding campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/8thdayofcreation/birthing-unity-and-peace-at-afrikaburn24).
**Come support the cause, get dressed up, engage in theatrical interactions, connect with your fellow burners and hangout in our fantasy themed spaces.** Get your tickets while they are hot!
**Venue:** Gorgeous George Hotel  
**Date:** 14 March 2024  
**Time:** 7 to 11 pm
