# AfrikaBurn Event Rules

**Last updated:** October 2024

Lost? You are here:
[Home](https://www.afrikaburn.org/) > AfrikaBurn Event Rules

### AfrikaBurn is an event where (almost) anything goes, but you still need to be responsible for yourself, while ensuring you’re also not affecting anyone else’s experience. It can be a hard line to tread when everything is ‘trickster this’ and ‘fu*k your burn that’, which is why, along with the guiding principles , we’ve put together the event policies and guidelines that will help you navigate (and get the most out of) Tankwa Town.

**Read through the list, and remember to check back as we get closer to the event! This page gets updated as we get closer…**

### Event Policies:

- In case you didn’t arrive via the link in that doc/page, please check out the [Event Disclaimer.](https://www.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-event-disclaimer/)
- [Ticket Purchase Agreement.](https://www.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-ticket-purchasing-agreement/) Which you’ve agreed to by purchasing a ticket.
- [Refund Policy.](https://www.afrikaburn.org/event-refund-policy/)
- [Photography and Media Policy](https://www.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-photography-media-policy/). For those who shoot people.
- [Supplier Policy.](https://www.afrikaburn.org/suppliers/) For those ordering decks.
- [Sound Policy](https://www.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-sound-policy/). For those who are spinning decks.
- [Generator policy](https://www.afrikaburn.org/afrikaburn-generator-policy/). For those with all the power.
- [eBike policy](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DMV-eBike-Policy-2020.pdf). For those who can’t go on their own steam.

### Event Guidelines:

- [Quaggapedia](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/) – which was once our Survival Guide.
- [First-timer’s checklist.](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/index.php?title=FIRST_TIME%3F) Worth a look if it’s your first time!
- [Health and safety tips](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/index.php?title=Main_Page). To keep you safe and healthy.
