# AfrikaBurn Pensioner Ticket Programme

#### For those members of our community who are 70 years plus chronologically, but much younger at heart.

**Send an email to[tickets@afrikaburn.com](mailto:tickets@AfrikaBurn.com) with a copy of your ID**, and your cellphone number, and we will allocate a pensioner ticket to you, along with the instructions on how to claim it.

#### You may need to get a younger person to help you with this! NOTE: This ticket is not transferable.
