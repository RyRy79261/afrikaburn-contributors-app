# Event Survey Online Town Hall

**We invite you to jump online for our next AfrikaBurn Town Hall, where we’ll be dialling you in on the highs, the lows, and all the in-betweens from this year’s Out Of The Blue Burn. With almost 100 slides of glorious participant information, we’ll be answering questions like …**

  * Where on Earth did everyone come from?
  * How many volunteers do you need to hold a fire perimeter?
  * What happened to all the Weekend Warriors?

This Town Hall is your chance to hear the voices and opinions of all who joined us in the dust, and not just the crew in your Zucker-Fuelled-Insta-Echo-Chamber.

AfrikaBurn 2025 Event Survey Town Hall

18 September, 7pm to 8:30pm
