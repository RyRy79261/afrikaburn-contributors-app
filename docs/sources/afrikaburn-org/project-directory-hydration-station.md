Hydration Station is calling all solo travelers and solo water lovers to join our tribe! 👫 We’re a super chill crew of hydration enthusiasts, of all ages ! 🌈

Our crib’s got the essentials: shade, community kitchen, and a basic shower to get you squeaky clean 🌿🚿. But here’s the magic: every volunteer brings 20L of fresh drinking water to share with the Burner fam! 🎁💧and 20l for personal use.

We’re all about spreading the hydration love, especially when the party’s raging and people forget to drink up 💦. Our midnight hydration patrols are legendary – we’ll hunt you down and make sure you’re good to go! 🕺💧

Join us, and let’s make some unforgettable memories together!
