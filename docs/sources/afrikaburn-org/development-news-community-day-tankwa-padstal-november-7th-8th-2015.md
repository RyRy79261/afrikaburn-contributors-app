We are pleased to present our [**report on the Community Day at Tankwa Padstal**](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Tankwa_2015_Final-report-1-1.pdf), held on November 7th and 8th, 2015. This event was a remarkable gathering that brought together residents, volunteers, and visitors from the Tankwa Karoo region and beyond. Over the course of two days, we engaged in activities that celebrated our shared values of community, collaboration, and cultural exchange. This report provides an overview of the event’s highlights, including workshops, performances, and collaborative projects that strengthened our community bonds. We extend our sincere gratitude to everyone who participated and contributed to making this event a success. We invite you to read through the report to revisit the memorable moments and accomplishments of those two special days.
