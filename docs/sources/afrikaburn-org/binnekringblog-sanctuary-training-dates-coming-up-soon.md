**If you are wondering if Sanctuary has a spot for you to volunteer this year,** please take a look at the [Quaggapedia page](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/index.php?title=SANCTUARY) for roles, and get in touch through the [Participation link](http://www.afrikaburn.com/the-event/participation/sanctuary)
We will be offering two Zooms and an in-person training session this year, before the event as follows:
  * ZOOM on 18th February and 25th March (both 10 am to 1 pm, latest, SA time)
  * In person at HQ on 15th April in Cape Town (10 am to 1 pm latest)

Take a look at your diaries and let us know which works best for you, if you haven’t already (so happy that so many signups are already in place – THANK YOU SO MUCH!). **How many more sleeps??? Exciting to get organised now!**
