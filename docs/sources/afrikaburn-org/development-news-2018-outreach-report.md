Here it is—the[ **AfrikaBurn Outreach Report for F18**](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Outreach-FY18-LR.pdf)! In a year where we had to pull up our frugal panties and get creative with resources, we still managed to spread the love far beyond the Binnekring. From hands-on workshops to uplifting local schools and supporting grassroots projects, we showed that a tight budget doesn’t cramp our community spirit. This report dives into the stories of resilience, radical collaboration, and what can be done when we stay true to our values—no matter the size of the purse strings. Dive in and feel the burn of frugal magic!
