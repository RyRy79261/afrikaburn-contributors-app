### NEED TO APPLY FOR A CREATIVE GRANT? NOW IS THE TIME!

Hello you dreamers, schemers, planners, players, performers, architects, artists, artisans, imaginers, creators, collaborators and celebrators …

[![Creative Grants are open!](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/creative-grants.jpg)](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/creative-grants.jpg)[  
](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/creative-grants.jpg)  
Are you dreaming up something wild and wonderful for Tankwa Town next year?

We know the imagination refuses to be bound by bank balances, and that sometimes wallets get a little light in the throws of radical self-expression. So if your plans are bigger than your budget and you’ll need some financial assistance for your project then you’ll want to apply for a creative grant – and NOW is the time!

Get your application in by **15 September** for an answer in **early October**.

Or, if you’re not quite ready yet, get your application in by **31 October** for an answer **mid November**.  
**(But be warned, Halloween is your last chance, so don’t delay.)**

To find out everything you need to know, consider, think about, and do, visit this link:

  * [APPLY FOR A CREATIVE GRANT](/participation-forms/apply-for-creative-grant "Creative Grant Application")

But don’t forget to complete one of these first …

  * [ARTWORKS](/participation-forms/register-artwork "Register an Artwork")
  * [PERFORMANCE OR EVENT](/participation-forms/register-performance "Register a Performance")
  * [THEME CAMPS](/participation-forms/register-theme-camp "Register a Theme Camp")
  * [MUTANT VEHICLES](/participation-forms/register-mutant-vehicle "Register a Mutant Vehicle")

**IF YOU’D LIKE TO APPLY FOR A CREATIVE GRANT AT LEAST ONE OF THE ABOVE FORMS MUST ACCOMPANY YOUR GRANT APPLICATION.**

Are you still reading this? Come, come, scamper scamper – away with you, you’ve got an application to fill out! Bring on the brilliance!
