# Naked Spectrum 2024

## **Beloved Burners! We are recruiting for the Naked Spectrum Art-Walk.** As a celebration of our human form, we paint ourselves into a naked, colourful parade that will take place on the Friday of the Burn at 12 noon at Hubble Bubble. This year, we are limiting numbers to around 50, and participants will be responsible for their own wash-up afterwards
Community-empowered Radical Self-Expression donning one very fine outfit: you and your fave colour! If this interests you or intrigues you but scares the pants off of you, so you may as well take part in any case, please email Matthew at [nakedspectrum@gmail.com](mailto:nakedspectrum@gmail.com) with your colour of choice and a backup colour. Colour choices are: Lime Green, Light Blue, Purple Passion, Cardinal Red and Orange Zest. We do ask for an R250 contribution towards your paint and application sponge. **Please confirm attendance by April 15th. We can’t wait to get colourful with you!”**
