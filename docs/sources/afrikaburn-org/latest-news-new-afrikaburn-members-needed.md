**On the rickety pirate ship that is AfrikaBurn, our wonderful Members sit up in the crow’s nest, keeping an eye on the horizon, ensuring that, as an organisation, we are heading in the right direction.**
All our Members are active in the AfrikaBurn community, meaning that they are committed to furthering the culture of AfrikaBurn through the 11 Guiding Principles in a year-round capacity. As such, the organisation seeks new Members to fill the gaps in the Membership that ensure cultural and cognitive diversity. The AfrikaBurn Membership is committed to being inclusive and equitable so that we can lead the way with experimental transformation. 
**If the idea of becoming a Member of AfrikaBurn appeals to you and you have enough time to spare in between swashbuckling and deck swabbing, please find out more about AfrikaBurn Membership and apply here:<https://www.afrikaburn.org/about/governance-and-transparency/how-afrikaburn-is-managed/>**
