# The Huggas and the little bookie nook

# **THE HUGGAS AND THE LITTLE BOOKIE NOOK**
### **Meet the Oxytocin Team – Home of the Huggas!**
If human connection is your art form and hugs are your medium, then you’ve found your tribe. At Huggas Camp, we celebrate warmth, joy, and the sneaky science of oxytocin – that little rush of happiness only a good hug can bring.
Every morning and afternoon, our apron‑clad Huggas prepare to top up the Binnekring with love. Step up to our “Hug Menu” and choose your embrace: gentle squeeze, bear hug, slow melt, or anything in between. Not seeing what you need? Order “off‑menu” – innovation is how new hugs are born! Whether you’re giving or receiving, you’ll leave a little lighter than before.
Our **Hug Lounge** is the heart of the camp – a soft, glowing nest of cushions, mats, fairy lights, and mellow music. It’s a place to land, recharge, and connect. Makers will find art supplies scattered about, because creativity often blooms when we relax together. The lounge stays open until the night’s last heartbeat drifts to sleep.
Tucked close to the centre is our **Bookie Nook** , a tiny sanctuary lined with pages and peace. It’s a quiet pocket for anyone needing to rest, reflect, or reset with a story. It’s open daily until 7 pm, perfect for a moment of stillness before the evening revelry begins.
We’re inviting new camp members who believe in the power of presence. If you love to greet strangers like friends, help hold emotional space, create little moments of magic, and keep the hugs flowing – this is your home at AfrikaBurn. Bring your energy, creativity, some camp love, and your best hugs. We’ll provide the rest.
