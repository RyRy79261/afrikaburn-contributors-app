## Poster: The Trickster
  * [ __ Other Author  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/otherauthor/)
  * [ __ March 4, 2014 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2014/03/04/)
  * __ 1:47 pm

  * __ Categories: [Art](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/creative-projects/art/)

Ladies and gentlenuts, please give a rousing welcome to…**our 2014 poster**! Yep, here it is, in all its multicoloured glory, replete with illustrated references to the tricksterish creatures of the Tankwa Karoo and a number of the iconic Clans, artworks and landmarks familiar to anyone who’s attended AfrikaBurn.
Massive gratitude goes out to our ever-gracious and clearly**talented artist, Alexis Aronson,** for her hard work and inspiring illustrations – it all adds up to a piece of work that brings this year’s Trickster theme to life beautifully. You can view more of Alexis’ work [here](http://alexisaronson.wix.com/intro).
Wanna view it in all its high-res glory? Just click on it – and to download the poster, do the ‘right click – Save As’ thing.
