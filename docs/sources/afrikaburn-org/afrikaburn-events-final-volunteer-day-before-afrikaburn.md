# FINAL VOLUNTEER DAY BEFORE AFRIKABURN!

This is it—**our last Volunteer Day before AfrikaBurn week!** If you’ve been waiting for the perfect chance to get involved, **this is your moment.**

**What We’ll Be Doing?****Screen Printing:**Help create rad designs to bring more color

**Capping LNT Buttplugs:**Locking in the final pieces of our Leave No Trace efforts.

**Clay Creations** : Give some color and life to our clay creations before we hit the dust!

**RFT Presence**: We will have some radio fun

This is the last push to **create, prep, and make sure everything is set** for AfrikaBurn. Whether you’ve been involved all along or are just jumping in now, **we need you!**

Bring your energy, creativity, and good vibes. Let’s build the Burn **one last time before the gates open!**

Tell a friend to tell a friend! See you there!
