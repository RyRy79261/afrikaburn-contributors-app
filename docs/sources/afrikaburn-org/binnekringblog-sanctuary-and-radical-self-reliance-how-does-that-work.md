**True radical self-reliance comes with self-awareness, right?** And self-awareness leads us to know what we need from others, right?
Sanctuary volunteering plays into our **Leave (A BETTER) No Trace principle.** You knew that, right? It’s not Leave NO Trace, it is Leave a BETTER Trace. Our Sanctuary guests leave us in a better place than when they arrive – they aim to help them feel connected, supported, loved and cared for. Often guests at Sanctuary come back to support others as they want to be a part of the magic unfolding; beautiful.
**Decommodification is not just about taking the labels off!** It is really about participating, rather than consuming our culture. Why not join the Sanctuary team to fully immerse yourself in volunteering and taking part, not watching?  
You can play a part in making the magic happen, rather than visiting the burn as an observer or a tourist, right?
