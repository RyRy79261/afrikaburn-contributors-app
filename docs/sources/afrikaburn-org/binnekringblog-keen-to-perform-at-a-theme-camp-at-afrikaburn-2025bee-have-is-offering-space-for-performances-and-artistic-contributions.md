**Discover the buzzing energy of BeeHave, a theme camp that celebrates community, creativity, and connection at AfrikaBurn 2025.** Step into a vibrant hive of artistic expression, freedom, and fun. Let your spirit take flight as you join us in our hexagonal oasis, designed to inspire and delight. BeeHave isn’t just a space—it’s an experience, where everyone can contribute to the magic, leaving with a renewed sense of belonging and purpose.
AfrikaBurn is a space where creativity thrives, and BeeHave takes that ethos to new heights. Our camp embodies the spirit of collaboration, inviting participants to immerse themselves in a dynamic world of movement, sound, and art. Whether you come to dance, learn, or simply connect, you’ll find that BeeHave is a place where boundaries dissolve, and the extraordinary becomes possible.
If interested to participate, please complete this form [[https://forms.gle/FgcDUXw7d98fT6iD7](_wp_link_placeholder)] by 31 March 2025.
