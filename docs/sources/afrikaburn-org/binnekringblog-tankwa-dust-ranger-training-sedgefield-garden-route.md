## 🔥🌵 Get ready to ignite your passion for community safety and radical self-reliance! 🚀 Join us for the ultimate Dust Ranger Training in the heart of Sedgefield, Garden Route, on March 16th!

📅 Date: March 16th, 2024  
🕘 Time: 9am – 1pm ish  
📍 Location: Sedgefield, Garden Route  
📧 Email [fanta@afrikaburn.com](mailto:fanta@afrikaburn.com) to secure your spot and get location details

Rangers play a crucial role in fostering connection amidst the harsh, isolated beauty of the Tankwa Karoo. They are Burners who volunteer in Tankwa Town, dedicating themselves to the safety and wellbeing of everyone. Their gift is time, skills, and energy in promotion of the 11 Principles.

To join the Rangers, you’ll need patience, empathy, good communication skills, and a positive attitude. Are you ready to step up and make a difference?

**In this training, we’ll cover:**  
✨ Deep Understanding of the 11 Principles  
✨ Health and Safety 101 (because safety is our jam!)  
✨ Conflict Resolution Skills (because sometimes things get as tangled as Burner hair!)  
✨ Approaches to Handling Situations Stickier Than Tankwa Dust

**Don’t miss out on this empowering experience! Secure your spot now and become a vital part of the AfrikaBurn community. Together, let’s create an awesome and memorable AfrikaBurn for all!**
