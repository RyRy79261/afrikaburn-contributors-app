## We are Take A Bus To AfrikaBurn, which has been assisting people getting to burn and back since 2014, saving fossil fuels and supporting the Afrika Burn community projects and artworks.
**We at Take A Bus To Burn have been escorting enthusiastic and exuberant, eco-friendly burners and burnadettes to this enchanting environment for the past nine earth years. Eager to support the ethos and envision the same ethics as AfrikaBurn we take great delight in assisting with the transportation needs of the eager attendees.**

### LATE MIDNIGHT EXPRESS: 2ND MAY 2024 23:45

### RETURN ONLY – ONE WAY Afrika Burn TO Cape Town  
Depart AB: Sunday 5th May 13:00
**Saving the planet one bus at a time is our moto and lowering the use of fossil fuels is our game!**  
**For Real! We want to save the earth and get YOU to experience the extraterrestrial environment that is Tankwa Town!**
This year we are supporting Camp Enos Nookie a cosy corner of the desert where intergalactic travellers can meet and intertwine with the expansive sound scapes they will be blasting off with.
Camp Enos Nookie:
Are you ready to blast off into outer space with us this year?!
“L-minus 45 seconds, Go For Launch!”  
“L-minus 3 seconds: “We have ignition!”  
“L-minus 0 seconds: LIFTOFF!”  
“We are headed to the Intergalactic Space Camp of Enos Nookie where you will experience and atmosphere like no other!”
“Enos” was a chimp who was the first of his species to leave earths atmosphere and orbit our planet Earth.  
“Nookie” is derived from the word nook meaning a secluded or sheltered place or corner.
**Enos Nookie is a corner where Space and Space Monkeys collide to bring some otherworldly soundscapes and visuals together****for the enjoyment of others.**
**Enos Nookie will be located at 12ish and C.**
**Come blast off with us!**
