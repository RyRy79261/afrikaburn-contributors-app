## Bombocart is a mutant vehicle bringing an old school wall of speakers on a small cart to the Burn. We’ll be playing D&B, Metal, Jungle, Breaks, etc. Anything but the “fluffy stuff”. But we need you help!
The trike we bought as the engine for our cart turns out to have a poked gearbox. We’re in the process of claiming our money back, but time is ticking for getting to this year’s Burn. So we desperately and urgently need funds to secure another vehicle (quad, trike, hovercraft).
  
The link to our crowdfunding page is on our blog: <https://bombocart.wordpress.com> as are our contact details in case you know any way you can help us out, for instance, you can gift us a quad bike, have a complete gearbox for a Yamaha YTM225 Tri Moto lying around, or have some old speakers or an amp we could use for our sound system.
