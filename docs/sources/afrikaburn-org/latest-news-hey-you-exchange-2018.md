## 2018 HEY YOU BURNER EXCHANGE

  * [ __ TravisLyle  ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/author/travislyle/)
  * [ __ November 23, 2017 ](https://www.afrikaburn.org/2017/11/23/)
  * __ 12:58 pm

  * __ Categories: [Latest News](https://www.afrikaburn.org/category/latest-news/)

Is your blood type A for Art? Were you born to perform? Does your wanderlust have you  
wondering where to from here? Well here it is Folks, the sign you’ve been waiting for –  
entries are officially open for the 2018 “Hey You! Burner Exchange” at the Lakes of Fire  
regional burn, just outside of Chicago, USA.  
One lucky artist will represent AfrikaBurn and take their creative project over to Lakes of  
Fire (June 13th – 17 th ). AfrikaBurn will cover the cost of your economy air ticket and visa application and provide R5 000 towards the cost of your project.  
CLOSING DATE FOR APPLICATIONS IS 23H59 ON SUNDAY 14 JANUARY 2018  
On the other side of the exchange you’ll get your ticket to Lakes of Fire at no cost and the  
Secret Gentleman’s Club will be your host camp, providing transport to and from the event, a full meal plan and your camping supplies. Also on offer is a place to crash both before and after the event, workshop space and tools. Midwest hospitality is a real thing!  
If this all sounds like the best idea you never had, get those creative juices flowing, and click on the link to apply:  
[**Apply as an artist for this year’s Hey You! Burner Exchange**](https://docs.google.com/a/afrikaburn.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdPONAM2mD_HoOGIRmrBYqWz-Z-_wWN-pUuUKb4PjQOjsUpJg/viewform?c=0&amp;w=1)  
For some extra inspiration (as if you needed it), check our the love-stories from the past  
year’s Exchange Burners:  
[Hey, You – Burner! by Duncan Larkin](https://www.afrikaburn.com/binnekringblog/hey-you-burner)[](http://www.afrikaburn.com/binnekringblog/wet-in-the-midwest-afrikaburn-on-exchange)  
[Wet In The Midwest by Braai Bliksem](http://www.afrikaburn.com/binnekringblog/wet-in-the-midwest-afrikaburn-on-exchange)  
And [here’s Rosey Posey’s take](https://www.facebook.com/groups/379235179892/permalink/10154544319759893/) on coming over to our thing in the desert.  
[Instagram album of last year’s event is here. ](http://www.instagram.com/lakes_of_fire/)
