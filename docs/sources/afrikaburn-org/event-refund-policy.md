# Refund Policy
Lost? You are here: [Home](https://www.afrikaburn.org/) > Refund Policy
**Last Updated:** 22 October 2025
**By purchasing this ticket, you agree to AfrikaBurn’s no-refund policy.**
**The ticket is a revocable license to attend AfrikaBurn. Accordingly, the Event Organiser reserves the absolute right, at its sole election and discretion, to revoke your licence to attend, at any time, for any amount of time, and for any reason.**
  * All sales of AfrikaBurn tickets and eTolls are final, and no refunds will be issued for any reason, including but not limited to: 
    * Where the event is terminated or cancelled due to harsh weather, acts of nature, government directions and/or regulations, or as a result of any other circumstance, including circumstances that are beyond AfrikaBurn’s control (force majeure);
    * If you are unable to attend and participate at AfrikaBurn for any reason whatsoever, including but not limited to, any reason related to a communicable disease, including COVID-19;
    * Failure to comply with the terms and conditions of this Agreement;
    * Where a license is revoked, for any reason whatsoever.
  * Tickets are fully transferable, except for Anathi, Subsidised, and Pensioner Tickets, and may be resold through the designated ticket resale platform for their face value, including any taxes, but less any admin charges incurred.
  * Ticket resales are only available via the official Quicket resale platform. Resales are only opened up when the event is completely sold out. AfrikaBurn is under no obligation to assist or facilitate resales of tickets.
  * AfrikaBurn does not guarantee the validity of any tickets purchased through other platforms or private sales, which is at your own risk.

### **Personal Insurance**
The Attendee acknowledges that the ticket price constitutes only a portion of the total costs of attending AfrikaBurn.
The Event Organiser recommends that all Attendees consider taking out personal insurance suited to their needs. This is particularly important for those travelling from overseas to attend AfrikaBurn.
It is the Attendee’s prerogative to obtain your own insurance cover to protect yourself in the event that you are unable to attend AfrikaBurn.
  1. You are hereby required to insure all property brought by you to the event, whether or not you own such property.
  2. In the instance that you require medical treatment, or to be medically evacuated as directed by medical professionals, you will be held liable for these costs. It is recommended that you have adequate medical insurance in place.
