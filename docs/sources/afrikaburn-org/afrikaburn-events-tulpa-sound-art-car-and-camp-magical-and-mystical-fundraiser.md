# Tulpa Sound / Art Car and Camp magical and mystical fundraiser
A 3-Day Weekender Fundraiser is happening in a secret location from **1 PM January 31 to 10 AM February 3**.
The event will host 300 attendees and feature three stages: the Full Tulpa Art Car as the main stage, the Chill River Stage, and the After Hours Boiler Room. Guests can enjoy pool parties, three sunsets and sunrises, and various workshops, including breathwork, mushy-yoga, dance, and sound healing.
International and local DJs will perform alongside surprise acts. Food and drinks will be available with buffet-style lunch and dinners, a full bar, a food truck, and coffee.
Tickets can be purchased [here](https://shop.eventix.io/29c5c04f-3a8f-4e1b-9687-bddacba95b60/tickets?shop_code=5u8s8a37), and Using the code “**davestories** ” grants you access to the Full Weekend Pass at a discounted rate.
