Yep, it's that time again folks - time to kickstart your imagination and start thinking about the project you're planning to bring to Tankwa Town next year. Got a plan for a wild Theme Camp? Keen to get greasy and ride a Mutant into the dust? Perhaps a performer? Maybe an artist with a crazy idea for something to build? It's open season on all of them - and Creative Grants and Mutant Vehicle Grants too!  
Just remember: in order to register any project, or apply for a Grant - and for that matter in order to buy tickets - you'd first need to register a Burner Bio. Don't have one? **[Create one here](http://new.afrikaburn.com/user/register)** so you can get the ball rolling. Got one already? No problem - you sir or madam can proceed, by using your Bio logins to unlock the magic.  
So, you're ready to register a project? Fantastic - hit the right link for your planned project:  
[Register a Theme Camp](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/register-theme-camp)  
[Register an Artwork](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/register-artwork)  
[Register a Performance](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/register-performance)  
[Apply for a Creative Grant](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/apply-for-creative-grant)  
[Register Mutant Vehicles](http://www.afrikaburn.com/the-event/participation/mutant-vehicles)  
[Apply for a Mutant Vehicle Grant](http://www.afrikaburn.com/participation-forms/apply-for-a-mutant-vehicle-grant/application-form)  
Don't forget - in order to apply for a Creative or Mutant Vehicle Grant, you need to register the project first, then apply for the Grant.  
We're looking forward to seeing what amazing amazing projects are being cooked up for 2017 - unleash the imagination!
