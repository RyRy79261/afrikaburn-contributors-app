Lastly, after braving the storms while camped on the Island of Rebar on the bank of Lake Tankwa and cut off by the Binnekring River, DPW would like to remind you that if you camp beyond the “No camping beyond this point” signs (as pictured below) you WILL drown. They have seen it. They know it. No jokes.
