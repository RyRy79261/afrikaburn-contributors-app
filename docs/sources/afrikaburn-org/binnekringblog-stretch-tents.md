## **Gather ‘Round: Community-Driven Shelter Solutions**
Are you seeking a reliable, cost effective eco-friendly, shelter solution for Afrikaburn? A creative collective is raising funds for their theme camp Hydration Station so this is a limited edition 2025.
Look no further! Their lightweight, all-weather Bedouin stretch tents offer a unique blend of traditional design and modern functionality.
## **Key Features:**
Durable, lightweight, water-resistant … UV protection stretch fabric.  
Easy setup and takedown.  
Spacious interior for gatherings and events  
Versatile design for various environments.
**Largest Size: 15m x 10m (inclusive of poles)**
**Price: R17000** 10x10m
By choosing their Bedouin stretch tent, you’re supporting Hydration Station, community-driven initiative and to supply drinking water to needy burners in the desert.
Contact them to learn more and reserve your tent today!  
The making of these special limited edition tents by a creative designer for burners to easily construct them…and transport them and providing much needed shade in the desert.
