## Help us transform this truck trailer into a solar-powered oasis to gift music, soft serve ice cream, and unforgettable moments in the desert!

_**Lick n Dab**_ is born from a deep love for AfrikaBurn and its principles of gifting, community, and creativity.

**The idea for Lick n Dab came from imagining what gift would bring delight to people braving the heat and elements of the Karoo desert.** What better way to bring smiles than a refreshing soft-serve ice cream served in a cozy, music-filled space? Paired with the laughter and connection of the AfrikaBurn community, it’s a simple yet powerful way to create joy. This project reflects everything we love about AfrikaBurn: creating magical moments, fostering connection, and gifting something meaningful to others.

[Find out more & support Lick&Dab](https://thundafund.africa/campaign/540F6F87C5B33B245B94)
