**LuminEssence, the AfrikaBurn 2024 Temple,** invites us on a journey to our inner selves, fostering deep self-acceptance and authentic love for ourselves, others, and creation. It encourages us to shine as our true selves with integrity in thought, feeling, speech, and action, offering a path of resonance, bliss, and genuine love.

**Join us at the Starry Night Soirée: An Odyssey of Art and Heart,** a fundraiser for the Temple, for an evening filled with captivating dance, soulful music, touching poetry, and elegant refreshments.

Dress up in your favourite steam punk, masked ball, or best Burn outfit for a chance to win a prize!

**Details:**  
**Sun Feb 18, 14:00 – 22:00**  
**23 DAVENTRY LANE, LONGLAKE EDGE**  
**LOGISTICS PARK, MODDERFONTEIN**

**Buy your tickets here:**  
<https://www.quicket.co.za/events/249205-luminessence-soiree/>

### 10% of proceeds will benefit the Home of Hope for Women and Girls, making your evening both magical and meaningful.
