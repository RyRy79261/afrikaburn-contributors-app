# Corrupting the next generation

## It’s that time of year, when we try to chat to as many art, design, architecture, fashion, drama and you-name-it creative students as possible, to get them inspired about AfrikaBurn and what lies within the opportunity to participate.

As a part of this programme, we offer any secondary or tertiary institution the opportunity for a member of the AfrikaBurn Ops team to come through and present mis-information about AfrikaBurn, and the blank canvas that all our events offer young creative minds. It’s also an opportunity to open up discussions about less-conventional jobs when students dream about their future … as our extended AfrikaBurn community is full of all sorts of strange artists, performers, architects and a million and one other exciting jobs you didn’t really know about when you were studying at school.

**If you work with, or know of a school or college that would possibly like to host us, and let us share the kool-aid with their young, impressionable minds (and talk about how much we love AfrikaBurn) for an hour, please get in touch with brain@afrikaburn.com**

## Each one, teach one!
