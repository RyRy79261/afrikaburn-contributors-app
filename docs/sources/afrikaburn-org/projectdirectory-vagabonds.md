## Project Description:
The Vagabonds is a growing international collective of burners who have organised theme camps at AfrikaBurn since 2019 and Borderland since 2022. In the default world, we are a diverse group of travelers, entrepreneurs, nomads, free spirits, creatives, and dreamers.
Initially inspired by the aesthetics of Swedish artist Simon Stålenhag and his Vagabond robots, this once Nordic-centric community has since grown to over 200 members who come to meet from all corners of the world to connect, co-create, and co-live.
We gather in the dust and dirt to celebrate life beyond the edges of civilization; to recharge our batteries, sweat out our inner demons, and dance like the universe is watching.

## Details of Gift:
🍁 Reflections Lounge: Our Scandinavian forest-themed lounge hosts workshops and massages by day and chill music by night, affording a cozy space to relax, connect, cuddle, and reflect, away from the madness (and the techno!). 
💆 Massages: Our signature daily massages are one of the Vagabonds’ most gratefully received gifts. From 11am to 2pm every day, we soothe dusty and dance-worn Burner bodies with a relaxing 10-15-minute massage.
☁️ Sky Lounge: Our twinkling Sky Lounge affords panoramic views of the playa without leaving the Binnekring or partying atop an Art Car (both of which you should also do). Sink into a floating cuddle puddle in our lounge nets or gaze out over the neon desertscape.
🪵 Woods in the Cabin Our inside-out cabin in the woods brings the forest wilderness indoors, transforming our (ex-)sauna into a cozy hideaway for intimate workshops and shared moments around a crackling fire.
🦄 Unicorn Room Our playful, unicorn-inspired space is brought back year after year by popular demand. Step inside the candy-coloured daydream to reconnect with your inner child – actual children are also welcome!
## Joining Requirements:
All camp members are expected to contribute to planning ahead of the Burn, on the ground, and afterwards. Build will start on Friday/Saturday and strike will go until Monday noon-ish.During the burn, camp shifts are shared evenly between camp members, and include cooking, cleanup, and other camp duties, as well as gifts like massages and hosting workshops.We also highly encourage all Vagabonds to take a shift at AfrikaBurn (Rangers, Sanctuary, etc).The camp fee will be $350-ish USD (TBD). 
## Additional Details:
We’re a camp of about 50 people with a shade structure to camp under, showers, electricity, a kitchen, and a private communal space where we enjoy a hot meal together every evening. It has everything you need to feel at home – cuddles, connection, and community. 😌
Find us on https://vagabonds.camp and instagram.com/vagabondsburn

The Crossing consists of two groupings of elements. A rigid line cuts through the landscape, a boundary that may represent law, structure, or control. Intersecting this line are more organic forms that rise irregularly, as though shaped by wind, erosion, or time. The line may be read as a border or a threshold, a boundary between order and chaos, captivity and freedom, life and death. Movement unfolds across this divide. From one perspective, the passage across the line could represent departure or exile, figures leaving something familiar behind. From another, the same movement may suggest arrival, a transition toward possibility and renewal. The Crossing comes together through shared effort, people learning, experimenting, problem-solving, and physically constructing something at scale. It exists because of a community, for a short time, shaped by the desert and the people within it. As with many desert installations, the sculpture is intended to transform through fire. When it ultimately burns, the work completes its own crossing, shifting from structure into flame, light, and ash.
