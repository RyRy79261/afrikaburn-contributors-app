## Perhaps, that greatest of treasures, YOU!
**Calling musicians of all kinds, poets of all shapes, and storytellers of all sizes – tall tales encouraged! Calling breathwork guides and yin yoga instructors. Calling MCs, actors, comedians, and facilitators.** We invite you to share your inner gifts with the good people of Tankwa Town by collaborating with us in our schedule of activities at Nowhere’s End. Where could you sprinkle your magic? We’re crafting late-night sound journeys, fireside storytelling, moonlit jams, and, of course, heartwarming cups of tea that restore faith in humanity.
**If this kind of collaboration gets your creative juices flowing, please email[ambassadorssofbliss@gmail.com](mailto:ambassadorssofbliss@gmail.com) to start the conversation; we would love to hear from YOU!**
