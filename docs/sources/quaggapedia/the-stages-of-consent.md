## The stages of consent culture for burn communities

> **How to use this model**
>
> Use this model as a way to assess where your community is in terms of building a healthy culture of consent.
>
> Use it as a tool when doing a temperature check on where your community is and then,
>
> Use it to implement extra strategies that your community could use in the phase that you are in, to move up the phases towards a more consent based culture.
