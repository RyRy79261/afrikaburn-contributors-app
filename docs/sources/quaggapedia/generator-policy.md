**Guidelines for Generators:**

Generator noise pollution can be more disturbing than a loud sound system. See also: [Fire Safety in your Camp](https://www.afrikaburn.org/binnekringblog/fire-safety-in-your-camp/).

## Guidelines
- Consider environmentally friendly energy solutions (solar, lower wattage appliances).
- Generator hours for Theme Camps and Artworks can't be regulated, but be as considerate as possible to neighbours.
- Record total generator fuel consumption in litres for Carbon Footprint calculations.
- Service your generator for maximum efficiency.
- Build a sound- and fire-resistant baffle for your generator to reduce noise.
- **Generator hours for campsites: 11am – 4pm.**
- Cables/leads are a trip hazard — handle safely.
- All generators should have a minimum 1.5kg fire extinguisher (DCP or CO2) close by.
- Sound systems in campsites should be battery operated.
- Sound systems in camping areas should be shut off after 11pm (a 12V battery is ample).
- Consider a blimped/silent generator or solar panels and batteries.
- **Mandatory switch-off times: 7am – 11am, Monday – Thursday** (includes generators, unless critical to Theme camp operations).

## Fuel Storage
- All flammable fuels (gas, petrol, diesel, paraffin) stored centrally in the campsite, under shade, at least 5m from sleeping/socialising areas and 10m from a fire. Suitable fire extinguisher nearby.
- There is a limit to fuel/gas quantity allowed per campsite.
- No fuel storage area closer than 30m from another fuel storage area.
- All fuel storage areas protected from vehicle collision; mark a 3m safety zone around stored fuel with caution tape or equivalent.
- Never fill a generator's tank while it is running.
