The 2022 Tankwa Town Map was designed by Kayla Grove.

Please note: The AfrikaBurn event site has rather large areas that are quite sandy and marked as best possible on the 2022 event map. These areas are great for building sandcastles and having a beach picnic, but not great for camps. It may look tempting but vehicles will get stuck and there's not enough hard ground to fix a camp to. Quaggafontein will be as windy as Stonehenge, so camping there risks tents becoming kites and meals gaining a "crunchy texture." **Please don't camp in these sandy areas** - there is enough space at Quaggafontein to build kraals on solid ground.
