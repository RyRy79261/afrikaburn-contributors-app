## Theme, Art, Mutant & Volunteers Camp Acculturation, Onboarding & Vetting Guidelines

**STAR** asked some experienced Camps as well as DPW & Volunteers to share some of their learnings with regards to ensuring Safety in their Camps and this is what they had to say:

### RAINBOW NUGGET #1 Have a Core Team of Leads / a Council

- Have a decision making body where issues can be raised, deliberated and agreed upon.
- Leads are clear & known by the whole camp.
- Code of conduct + principles are clear.
- Camp members are approved by core group or a buddy system.
- Follow your intuition with regards to camp members.

![Approved image.png](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/index.php?title=Special:Redirect/file/Approved%20image.png)

### RAINBOW NUGGET #2 Create an online application form

- Outline expectations
- Outline camp fees
- Obtain Key personal info (including ID details + Photo)
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Reference Check Questions
- Get Emergency Contacts
- Mental Health/substance/medical issues - for accountability + individual safety
- Direct to [AfrikaBurn Website](https://www.afrikaburn.org/our-horizon/our-guiding-principles/), Quaggapedia discuss / share topics i.e. Consent, AfrikaBurn Principles etc.

**Get to know your applicants:**

- Ask them if any concerns about being out there.
- Ask them to share a joke
- Ask them to share their favourite AfrikaBurn principle

### RAINBOW NUGGET #3 Clearly outline your Eviction Policy

For example: CEXx provides a safe and inclusive space, infringements will result is eviction from the camp and will result in involvement with Rangers and may lead to possible eviction from the event.

**CEXx has Zero Tolerance for**

- Violence
- Consent Violations
- Discrimination on ANY grounds
- Harassment

### RAINBOW NUGGET #4 Review Application + Interview

Key points:

- Are they a good culture fit for your camp, are they aware of, and will they meet your camp needs?
- Do Reference Checks with other Camps / Volunteers.
- Work together with other Portfolio Camps.
- Create a report card to ensure visibility with other camp leads and decision making.
- **Listen to your intuition.**
- Aim to create a cohesive and diverse crew.

### RAINBOW NUGGET #5 Fundraisers / socials / communication groups are opportunities for:

- Vetting, acculturation and onboarding.
- Teaching about ethos + consent approach in practical ways and, practice working together as a crew and handling challenging situations.
- Applying the AfrikaBurn principles so that the crew are building those principles into their preparation for the dust.
- Chat Groups: introductions + photo in drag or Burn attire - Opportunity to create a space of non judgment and radical expression.
- Chat Groups: Discuss / share topics i.e. Consent, AB Principles etc.
- A culture fit check.

### RAINBOW NUGGET #6 Onsite Onboarding

- DO Introductions (esp. highlighting safety monitors and camp leads), SET expectations, EXPLAIN camp works day-to-day, and do a Q&A.
- Outline Safety in camp, consent etc.
- Explain LNT, Toilets etc.
- Basics around sharing an environment with others.
- Story time/talent show/kak praat—builds community and allows camp leads to become aware of and take action where necessary.
- Morning camp meeting opportunity to deal with issues etc.
- Hot tip: Encourage training & volunteering for Rangers, Sanctuary, Volunteers etc. These are such great culture learning opportunities.
- Encourage the reporting of red flags to leads or Safety Monitors with immediacy.

### RAINBOW NUGGET #7 Camp Services & Gifts

- Ensure review/referrals & approval of service providers (esp. where touch could be involved e.g. yoga, massage, etc.)
- Daily Check-in's with crew regarding any red flags no matter how subtle.
- Camp Leads are approachable.
- Identify and report problems early.
- Safety Monitors on duty, multiple and visible. Visible monitoring makes a massive difference to maintaining a safer space.
- STAR are there to support you if you need assistance with red flags.

### RAINBOW NUGGET #8 Camp Handbook

Some camps create handbooks for their crew with info about Camp Leads, crew, offerings and special events. You can also include the principles, info about the toilets, LNT etc.

### RAINBOW NUGGET #9 Civic Responsibility & The Buddy System

- Some camps opt for Camp members to take responsibility for onboarding, vetting and issues similar to buddy system / champions
- Everyone is accountable for everyone and themselves

#### Champions / Buddy System

- The Buddy System says, "this is a good person and I take responsibility for them when they flail or fail to show up."
- They must be someone you know.
- Everyone is recommended and responsible to one another.
- Example situations where the Champion should jump in: flailing on a camp shift / conflict with another camper / accompanying them back to camp or to the hospital for a medical problem
- *Consider: Who am I responsible for? Who is responsible for me?*

### RAINBOW NUGGET #10 ALSO...

- Have a verbal discussion about consent and safety onsite.
- When complaints: get a read on the issues and to do a temperature check. Individuals should not be made to be responsible for the ensuring the culture safety.
- Let STAR know of any incidences / complaints onsite or post event.
- Everyone is responsible for upholding a culture of safety.
- Participants can always come and talk to STAR (through Rangers / Sanctuary or offsite on star@afrikaburn.com).
- Post event, **evaluate** yourselves and crew through surveys and come up with **improvements** and ideas for next year.
