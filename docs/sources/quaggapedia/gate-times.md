## What documentation will you need to access the event?
Gate Crew checks **photo ID** against ticket details — ID/Passport or Driver's License accepted (clear copy of photo ID also accepted). Ensure your ID/Passport number is correct on the ticket for everyone travelling with you.

When purchasing tickets, ensure correct **emergency contact details** for someone off- and on-site — the Box Office crew links your uniquely numbered wristband to your details in the system for emergencies.

Vehicles need an **E-Toll disk**, purchasable at the gate until gates close at 7pm Friday. E-Tolls are unlimited and won't sell out.

## For the Best Gate Experience — come prepared
Print your ticket or ensure device battery. Make sure ID/Passport details match the ticket details — if not, check edit details & transfer ticket info in the [Ticketing FAQs](https://tickets.afrikaburn.org/#FAQ).

## Early Arrivals and WAPs
**Early Arrival**: requires a **Work Access Pass (WAP)** from your creative project lead or the Volunteer Portfolio to enter before official gate opening. Kids/minors don't need a WAP but need a parent/guardian.

WAPs include a Vehicle Pass, but WAPs won't be accepted as a Vehicle Pass after Sunday 24th April. Crew passes include a WAP and Vehicle Pass.
