**[Tankwa Town map for 2024 is right here](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/images/f/f7/AFRIKABURN_2024_MAP_FINAL_A4.pdf)!**

A copy will be available at the gate and at the main notice board at Off-Centre Camp (OCC).

Please note: The AfrikaBurn event site has rather large areas that are quite sandy, marked as best as possible on the event map. These areas are great for building sandcastles and having a beach picnic, but not great for camps. It may look tempting but vehicles will get stuck and there's not enough hard ground to fix a camp to. **Please don't camp in these sandy areas.** There is enough space at Quaggafontein to build kraals on solid ground.
