### Binnekring
The Binnekring is the centre of Tankwa Town. Translated from Afrikaans it means 'inner circle'. In Black Rock City they call it 'The Playa'. As AfrikaBurn is in the Karoo and not the USA, we prefer The Binnekring.

### Boskak
A boskak comes from South African slang meaning to relieve oneself in nature. It literally means 'bush shit' and occasionally happens in the Binnekring, which is horrific for someone to have to clean up. There are toilets at the event site — rather use those.

### Darkwad
A Darkwad is a person who walks or cycles at night without any lights. This can be extremely dangerous as there are bicycles and mutant vehicles driving around that will not be able to see these people and possibly bump into them. Light yourself and your bicycle up at night by using LEDs, head lamps, EL-Wire or LED Flowtoys.

### Don't be kak, be lekker
Refers to a conscious way of being. Lekker comes from Afrikaans, referring to a person, object or event as amazing, fantastic or superb. Being a lekker person will make both your and others' burn an even better event. Ways to be lekker: be kind, be conscious, leave no trace, be positive, be creative, love.

### Ground Score
When one finds something exciting that doesn't belong in Lost and Found. A reward for those who collect MOOP.

### Humanure
Human excrement that is recycled via composting for agricultural or other purposes. The toilets in Tankwa Town are completely eco-friendly — what goes into the toilets returns to the Earth. The rule: if it did not naturally come out of your body, please do not put it there (except single-ply loo paper).

### (ish)
Time moves differently in Tankwa Town. "Ish" refers to a broader sense of time — African Time — a more relaxed attitude vs. clock-bound Western pace. No timetable or schedule for any event during the burn; times stipulated are purely estimates.

### MOOP
Matter Out Of Place — everything that doesn't belong in the Tankwa Karoo desert (litter, feathers, glitter, etc.). Relates to the Leave No Trace principle.

### Plug and Play
Paying someone a (large) sum of money to set up all your comforts for you. Plug and Play camps are against the Guiding Principles of AfrikaBurn (violates Radical Self-Reliance and Communal Effort) and are strongly discouraged/frowned upon.

### Right now, now-now, just now
"Right now" is this very moment, "now-now" means shortly, "just now" means later to never.

### San Clan
AfrikaBurn's main sculpture, analogous to the Man at Burning Man. Design originates from a San cave painting from Tarkastad depicting one body, several heads and many dancing feet — portraying unity, collaboration and community. It is burnt, usually on Saturday night.

### SOOP
Sound Out Of Place — sound pollution. AfrikaBurn has a sound policy in place to balance radical self-expression with community, keeping SOOP to a healthy limit.

### Sparkle Pony
A high-maintenance person who comes to the burn unprepared for the harsh environment, often scantily dressed with MOOPy accessories, many outfits but little water, relying on others for basic needs — throws radical self-reliance out the window.

### The Tim
It's all his fault.

### Veteran
Someone who's done it all, seen it all, and gotten at least one t-shirt.

### Virgin
A first-time ticket holder.

### Weekend Warriors
People who only arrive Thursday/Friday to party hard over the weekend, mostly consuming without contributing much. Encouraged to volunteer if only attending the weekend.
