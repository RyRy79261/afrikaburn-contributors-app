## **Helplines**

### National GBV helpline
For reporting incidents of gender-based violence. Alternatively, you can send a please call me by dialling *120*7867# for counselling and other services, including shelter.

Helpline: 0800 428 428

### Domestic violence helpline
24/7 toll free helpline for reporting domestic violence including sexual, physical, emotional, and financial abuse. The helpline is also accessible via WhatsApp.

Helpline: 0800 150 150

### Thuthuzela Care Centres
Thuthuzela Care Centres (TCCs) are one-stop facilities based in hospitals aimed to reduce secondary trauma. TCCs offer designated forensic and medical service available to rape survivors as an emergency service in the first 72 hours after a rape. There are TCCs across all 9 provinces in South Africa.

www.gov.za/TCC

### [Rape Crisis Cape Town Trust](https://www.facebook.com/rapecrisiscapetown)
Rape Crisis Cape Town Trust offers support, access to the criminal justice system, counselling to rape survivors & victims of sexual offences.

Helpline: 021 447 9762 / 021 447 1467 / communications@rapecrisis.org.za

### Childline South Africa
This non-profit organisation helps abused children and their families with free counselling service. It deals with issues such as physical and sexual abuse, trafficking, and gives legal advice.

www.childlinesa.org.za | Toll-free helpline: 116

### Tears Foundation
TEARS Foundation provides access to crisis intervention, advocacy, counselling, and prevention education services for those impacted by sexual assault and child sexual abuse.

www.tears.co.za | Free SMS helpline: *134*7355#
