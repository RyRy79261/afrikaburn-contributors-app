**OTHER THAN THE SCHEDULED BURNS OF ARTWORKS, THE FOCUS OF FIRE CONTROL IN TANKWA TOWN IS AVOIDANCE, AWARENESS AND ACTIVE PREVENTION.**

If something catches on fire, we will get people out of harm's way and then probably just let it burn out – Rangers and Fire Safety crews protect people, not property.

Be prepared: **bring adequate fire extinguishers and know how to use them** — practice Pull, Aim, Squeeze and Sweep (P.A.S.S.). In the case of tent or structure fires, DO NOT attempt to enter the tent/structure to remove possessions.

When planning your camp, leave at least three metres between flammable items (e.g. tent 3m from generator, generator 5m from fuel — applies to cars/campervans too). **All camps must have a fire extinguisher on hand and visible.**

- No fires directly on the ground — braais and braziers must be used.
- Fires can't be left unattended — must be put out if not in use.
- Unapproved artworks may not be burned. To burn something you've created, contact the Art crew via the Arteria at Off-Centre Camp.
- When burning structures, keep a safety perimeter equal to three times the sculpture's height (more if windy), marked with lights/tape/safe markers.

## CAMP SAFETY
Don't store too much gas; store any gas at least 10m away from your main camp activity, in shade, not direct sunlight. Petrol at camp must be minimal, with signage indicating fuel on site.

**TIP:** at least 2 fire extinguishers per 50sqm area; a fire blanket close by if cooking with oil; every camp should have a 5-litre bucket of sand.

For large gas/fuel storage (camps, Mutant Vehicles, Artworks), contact the Fire Safety Officer (at OCC) to request storage at the fuel dump.

## FIRE LANTERNS AND FLARES
Not permitted — serious fire hazard, problems with neighbouring farms and Tankwa Karoo National Park, and lanterns become snares for small mammals/reptiles. **LABEL YOUR FUEL CONTAINERS.**

## FIRE SAFETY

**Caravans/RVs and bottled gas**
- Always change cylinders in open air, away from ignition sources.
- Check connections with soapy water/brush (no bubbles = no leak).
- Turn off at main valve when not in use; keep cylinders outside caravan.
- Turn off all appliances before bed/leaving (unless designed to run continuously, e.g. fridge).
- Never use a cooker/heater while caravan is moving.

**If you suspect a leak:** turn off all appliances, open doors/windows, do not smoke, do not operate electrical switches.

**Other hazards:** never leave cooking/open flame unattended; keep matches/lighters from children; use metal ashtrays if smoking; never smoke in bed; don't leave children alone in a caravan; keep caravan ventilated.

**Tents**
- Allow safe spacing between tents, caravans, cars.
- Never use candles/open flame in or near a tent — use torches.
- Keep flammable liquids and LPG cylinders outside the tent in labelled containers; excess fuel goes to the Fuel Dump (ask at OCC).
- Oil-burning appliances not recommended; cooking appliances not for small tents; keep away from walls/roof.

## OPEN FIRES
**Open fires on the ground are NOT PERMITTED.**

- Fire must be on a raised platform, downwind, at least 10m from any tent.
- Clear wood/leaves/tinder to form a circle of clear earth around the fire.
- Build a stack that collapses inward; ideally not higher than the fireplace's sides.
- Do not leave fires unattended — fully extinguish before leaving.
- Keep a 5-litre bucket of sand and fire extinguisher(s) nearby.

## If a fire breaks out
- Contact Ranger, Security, Medics or Fire Branch, give exact location (street reference or landmark like a Theme Camp/art piece).
- Then remove objects (tents, wood, vehicles, etc.) from the vicinity if safe to do so.

## Guidelines for open fires, burn barrels, bamboo torches within Theme Camps
- Open fires on the ground aren't permitted — use a brazier/braai.
- Burn barrels/braais can't be left unattended — will be put out if found so.
- Designate a camp member as Safety Baron responsible for fire safety and locating extinguishers.
- If winds pick up, all open fires must be put out immediately; burn barrels extinguished if throwing sparks.
- Must be extinguished at request of any Ranger/Emergency Services personnel.
- A 5m zone around any fire must be free of flammable materials (cloth, paper, tents, plastic, etc.).
- All liquid fuels kept at least 10m from any open flame, carefully labelled (3 past incidents of people using fuel cans to try to put out fires).
- At least 25 litres of water (and/or fire extinguisher) kept on hand.
- Burn barrels secured/constructed so burning surface is at least 15cm from the ground.

## Fuel Storage
Guidelines when planning your camp:
- Tent to fuel: 5 metres
- Tent to fire: 5 metres
- Fuel to fire: 10 metres (placed in a triangle)
- Applies to cars/camper-vans too — keep 5m+ from fuel or fire.
- Camps should have a 5-litre bucket of sand plus fire extinguisher.
- All flammable fuels (gas, petrol, diesel, paraffin) stored centrally, under shade, furthest from sleeping/socialising/fire area, with a suitable extinguisher nearby.
- **1 x 4.5kg DCP (Dry Chemical Powder) or 1 x 5kg CO2 fire extinguisher per 8x8m of covered area** (e.g. 4 medium tents/awning/gazebo). Placed centrally, accessible, visible; check dates and gauge (needle in green).

Petrol is dangerous for starting fires (explosive vapours can flash). Use broken-up candles as fire-lighter alternative.
