**Just In(e), is our Lost & Found hut that can be found at OCC (Off-Centre Camp) during the event.**

Anything not claimed by the end of the event will be brought back to our Cape Town HQ and we will begin sorting and categorising when we've recovered from the event itself.

All this information and all the photographs will then be posted on our [Lost & Found Group on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/426687820074870).

This is not an easy process, so please be patient.

For more direct communication with our Lost & Found Team, you can send an email to lostandfound@afrikaburn.com with a clear description of the item, as well as any further information (photos, sketches, serial number, etc.) and they will communicate with you via email.

All items not positively identified will be donated to charities through our outreach programme, and all bicycles not collected will be donated to the communities surrounding Tankwa Town and previously disadvantaged communities in Cape Town.

## TOP TIPS

**BIKES**: Label your bikes!

**PHONES:**

- Set your phone's lock screen text or background image to a picture of your camp, the camp's location, and your name. **Your full name is critical information**.
- If your phone is handed into Lost and Found, they will **not** be able to deliver the phone to your camp. Please check in with the Lost and Found booth.
- Fill out your Medical ID, Emergency contacts, and medical information on your phone before the burn. First responders can find your info on your phone even if the screen is locked. Under settings on your Android phone, search for '*Emergency*' and on iPhones look for '*Medical ID*'.
- If the AfrikaBurn lost and found volunteers are able to locate a name on your phone's emergency screen, they will be able to search the ticketing database to find your details.

**OTHER PERSONAL ITEMS:**

Leave your precious things at home or in your tent/car but if you need it with you, please label it with your name or number.

If you find anything in your moop sweeps, please bring it to the Lost and Found Booth before you leave.
