**Gifting is one of the 11 Guiding Principles Burners strive to follow.**

![Gifting.jpg](https://quaggapedia.afrikaburn.com/index.php?title=Special:Redirect/file/Gifting.jpg)

A gift is offered unconditionally, without expectation of anything in return. It can be big or small, but its impact depends on you.

If you're unsure what to bring, remember **the best gifts are not things** — they're moments of magic and lasting memories.

**Advice for virgin burners:** don't stress about what to gift your first year — just bring yourself, be present, and lend a hand where you can. Sign up for volunteer shifts.

Consider the repercussions of a gift once given: e.g. beaded necklace strings that snap become MOOP; orange peels need somewhere to go. Consider gifting time, labour, love, assistance, a plaster, iced water, an experience, a game, a song, or simply acknowledging another's existence.

If gifting foodstuffs, disclose ingredients (allergies are real) and disclose any added substances (e.g. alcohol) — **consent is key**, always, so the recipient can make an informed decision.

The recipient is free to accept or decline a gift — be gracious about refusal and offer it to someone else.
