**Your AfrikaBurn experience will be made all the easier and enjoyable if you seriously consider where you're going, what you're doing and what you'll need to do it.**

AfrikaBurn is not a usual festival — it's an exercise in creativity and survival in an extreme environment. Plan ahead, plan smart, bring everything you need.

[Excellent blog from a 2019 participant - Bren On The Road](https://brenontheroad.com/surviving-afrikaburn/)

## Essentials
- 5 litres of water per person per day (drinking, washing & cleaning) – carry a bottle and rehydrate regularly.
- Food and beverages for your entire party/stay – nothing on sale but ice.
- A good, basic first aid kit.
- Shelter: windproof tent with bedding, preferably an open shade structure too.
- Camping lights, torches, headlamps, LEDs + spare batteries.
- Rubbish bags – you take it all back with you.
- Hand sanitiser.
- Protective clothing (warm clothes and waterproof gear — hot days, freezing nights, rain possible).
- Good closed shoes/hiking boots and enough clean socks.

## Leave at home
- Plants, feathers (e.g. boas that shed), glitter, excess packaging.
- Fireworks, sparklers, Chinese lanterns.
- Explosives or firearms, including BB/pellet guns.
- Pets of any description.
- Don't overstock fresh produce.

## Highly recommended
- Umbrellas, parasols, hats, sunscreen, lip balm, sunglasses.
- Bicycle (mountain bikes best), decorated/lit up, with puncture kit.
- Portable ashtray for smokers — no butts on the ground.
- A portable shower.
- Tennis balls etc. to blunt sharp tent pegs.
- An extra set of car keys kept separately.
- Costumes, instruments, signs, body paint.

## Toilets
- Only human waste and one-ply toilet paper in Throne toilets (waste is converted to manure).
- Tampon bins in toilets marked with a red triangle.
- Always close the lid when done (keeps flies/odours out).
- Do not dump grey water/personal camping-toilet bags in toilets — use an evaporation pond or transport off-site.
- NO TAMPONS, RUBBISH OR WET-WIPES in the toilets.

## Camping
- No camping beyond the inner edge (Binnekring) or bush line. Registered project-based (theme) camps get a reserved placement location; all other camps use Open Camping (first-come-first-camped).
- On arrival, visit Off-Centre Camp for Ranger HQ, Medics, Volunteer Booth, Kid's Registration, etc.
- FUEL: no large amounts of liquid/gas fuel stored in campsites — use Fire Safety guidance.
- Plug & Play camps (paid staff, run for profit, set up for 'clients') are not permitted.
- Theme camps using delivered services/infrastructure should check the **Supplier Process** — see Supplier Depot.
- Contact a Ranger (orange vest, or at Off-Centre Camp) for camp-area problems.

## Cars
- Pedestrians and bicycles only; art cars/emergency vehicles excepted. Vehicles parked from arrival until departure.
- **SPEED LIMIT IS 10KMH.**

## Commerce
- Nothing to buy/sell except **ice**. AfrikaBurn is a decommodified gift-economy zone; be self-reliant.
- No promoting/marketing branded products, or photographing/filming products/services at the event.

## Fire
- Bring a fire extinguisher and 5-litre bucket of sand; tell campmates where these are.
- Campfires are your responsibility — **never leave a fire unattended**.
- Open fires on the ground not permitted; extinguish braai/brazier fires before leaving camp.

## Leave No Trace
- AfrikaBurn is a Leave No Trace event — everything you bring, you take away.
- Applies on the road to/from the event too — take rubbish with you at any stops; don't leave MOOP bags at the gate or roadside on departure.

## Some Extra Tips
- Bring beer/soda in cans (flatten easily for MOOP transport).
- Bring an old rug for your tent entrance to reduce sand tracked in.
- Bring at least two pairs of broken-in shoes.
- Keep a clean, dust-free set of clothes in your vehicle for the drive home.
- Label any bike you bring with name and contact number.
