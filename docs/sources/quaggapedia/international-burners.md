Over half of ticket holders come from overseas. This page covers extra challenges: getting from abroad to Tankwa Town and preparing supplies for the desert.

## The runway to Tankwa Town

### 1. Research Stage: Understanding AfrikaBurn
AfrikaBurn is not a music festival — it's a co-creation between all participants; there are no spectators. Read the Guiding Principles (eleven principles). Learn about the Gifting culture and Basics 'rules'. Also see the [AfrikaBurn Resource Community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/resources.africaburn/) (Facebook).

### 2. What experience are you hoping for?
Think about whether you're coming solo, with friends, or family — preparation differs. Consider whether to volunteer, create art, join/set up a theme camp, or be self-reliant. You don't have to join a theme camp.

### 3. Getting ready before you leave home
See "Before You Go, Prepare" and "First Time?" pages, plus the FAQs.

### 4. When you land in South Africa
From Cape Town International, Quaggafontein is about 4-5 hours' drive. From Johannesburg International, it's an epic 1200km+ road trip, usually done in 2+ days. Burner-friendly guest houses exist along the route — ask in the [Q&A Space for Burners](https://www.facebook.com/groups/burn.community.info/) Facebook group.

### 5. Road to the Burn
Expect a portion of the infamous R355, the longest gravel road in South Africa — see the Getting There & Directions page; people have died on this road. Stop at Padstals (farm stalls) along the way for wifi, coffee, tyre repair, supplies, and to support the local community.

### 6. Arrival in Tankwa Town
See "On Arrival" page.

### 7. Setup Camp
See "Camping" page.

### 8. The Burn itself
Tankwa Town is laid out like a melted clock face. The Binnekring (inner ring) hosts theme camps (drinks, dancefloors, workshops, showers, performances). Inside the Binnekring are most artworks — explore before and as they burn. Mutant vehicles roam the Binnekring day and night — bring lights, it gets dark.

Toilets ("Throne" toilets) are a notable pleasant experience — always close lid and door.

AfrikaBurn parallels Burning Man but with its own context: Off-Centre Camp (vs Centre Camp), burning "the Clan" (vs the Man, referencing San/Bushmen rock art).

The 11th principle, Each One Teach One, means being patient with fellow burners who may not know everything. Gift with consent and MOOP in mind. Label/lock bikes; turn found bikes in to Lost and Found. Rest, drink water; go to the medical tent or The Sanctuary at Off-Centre Camp if unwell.

### 9. Exodus
Leave your camp spotless — pick up every piece of MOOP. Unopened non-perishables/sealed water that can't travel home can go to [Collexodus](https://www.afrikaburn.org/binnekringblog/collexodus/), supporting DPW (Department of Public Works) crews. Drive slowly and carefully home.

### 10. Decompression
Effects of the Burn linger — consider a road trip to ease back into the default world rather than flying out immediately. A Burner Bar gathering typically happens in Cape Town soon after the event.

### 11. Living the Principles beyond the Burn
Continue applying the Guiding Principles in the default world. Consider donating good-quality camping equipment to charity or fellow burners before leaving South Africa.

## FAQ

### Renting an RV
Convenient but expensive and often booked out well in advance; many rental companies don't allow their vehicles to go to AfrikaBurn or travel on dirt/gravel roads.

### Getting the Burner Bus
'Bus to AfrikaBurn' is a participant-created (not official) service — see the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/BUStoAfrikaBURN/).

### Bare minimum camping
**You will definitely need:** waterproof tent, mattress, sleeping bag & pillow, earplugs, gas burner or similar, pot/kettle, basic cutlery/cups/plates, chairs and table, washing bowl, head torch, basic first aid kit.

**Nice to have:** gazebo/shade awning, BBQ or braai, cooler box (only ICE is sold at the burn).

Buy equipment responsibly and support local businesses; Cape Town's second-hand market is also strong. Consider donating good camping equipment to charity after the event.

### A note about Plug & Play
Joining an existing theme camp for money in exchange for comfort is discouraged — these "Plug & Play" arrangements dilute the AfrikaBurn experience; some participants have paid for promised showers/meals and received little. See [Plug & Play guidelines](https://www.afrikaburn.org/plug-and-play-guidelines/) and the [Plug and Play Flow Chart](https://www.afrikaburn.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/AB_PP-theme-camp-flow-chart-final.pdf).
