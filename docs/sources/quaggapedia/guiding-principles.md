AfrikaBurn is an official Burning Man regional event, held in the Tankwa Karoo, Western Cape, South Africa since 2007. Centred on temporary artworks in a semi-desert environment, some burnt towards the event's end. The Guiding Principles stem directly from [Burning Man's 10 Principles](https://burningman.org/about/10-principles/).

## COMMUNAL EFFORT
Values creative cooperation and collaboration; strives to produce, promote and protect social networks, public spaces, artworks, and communication methods that support interaction.

## CIVIC RESPONSIBILITY
Values civil society. Organisers should assume responsibility for public welfare, communicate civic responsibilities to participants, and conduct events per national/local laws.

## DECOMMODIFICATION
To preserve the spirit of gifting, seeks social environments unmediated by commercial sponsorship, transactions or advertising. Resists substitution of consumption for participatory experience.

## PARTICIPATION
The real magic lies in participation — a radically participatory ethic. Transformative change occurs only through deeply personal participation. Everyone is invited to work and play.

## IMMEDIACY
Immediate experience is the most important touchstone of value; seeks to overcome barriers to recognition of inner selves, others' reality, participation in society, and contact with the natural world.

## GIFTING
Devoted to acts of gift giving; value of a gift is unconditional, with no expectation of return or exchange of equal value.

## LEAVING NO TRACE
Respects the environment; committed to leaving no physical trace of activities, cleaning up, and leaving places better than found. MOOP (matter out of place) examples: cigarette butts, glowstick connectors, matches, bottle caps, string, glitter, toilet paper, shells, batteries, broken LEDs, feather boa scraps, food scraps, broken glass, tape, sequins, false eyelashes, lighters, and more.

## RADICAL SELF-RELIANCE
Encourages individuals to discover, exercise, and rely on their inner resources.

## RADICAL SELF-EXPRESSION
Arises from the unique gifts of the individual; only the individual (or collaborating group) determines its content; offered as a gift to others, respecting the recipient's rights and liberties.

## RADICAL INCLUSION
Everyone should be able to be part of AfrikaBurn; actively pursues mechanisms to address imbalances and barriers to participation, especially given past/current/systemic injustice. Welcomes and respects strangers.

## EACH ONE TEACH ONE
As a self-reliant community, spreading culture is everyone's responsibility — knowledge is passed on when the opportunity presents itself.

---
These principles aren't commandments or rules — they are ideals that offer guidelines for reinventing the world and ourselves, enhancing experience in Quaggafontein and living in harmony with fellow burners. In summary: don't be kak, just be lekker.
