## RULE ONE: Keep to the safe speed limit of 70km/h
The R355 is a deceptive dirt road that has claimed many lives. Drive calmly even with a 4x4. The last stretch is a narrow farm road — be patient, there may be a queue.

## RULE TWO: Don't just follow the GPS coordinates
GPS coordinates for the entrance: **32°28'57.5"S 19°53'51.4"E**, or type 'Quaggakop' into your GPS app (NOT Quaggafontein). Some GPS apps route incorrectly (e.g. via Ceres, or through other farms). Don't click unofficial map links without checking.

## How to survive the R355
- Stick to 70km/h (or less); don't overtake if you can't see ahead.
- Normal road safety applies, even to RVs and 4x4s.
- Travel in convoy if possible for tyre-change help.
- Drive during daylight hours.
- Don't overload your vehicle.
- Reduce speed to 50km/h or less if heavily loaded.
- Drive at dawn — cooler tyres/roads mean fewer punctures.
- The dirt section takes about 1.5 hours and is the most treacherous part.
- Cellphone coverage is patchy; last reliable signal just before Karoopoort (from south) or Bloukrans Pass (from north).
- Don't stop on a blind rise if you pull over.

### Option 2 — alternative route avoiding much of the R355
- From Ceres: MR310/R303 via Prince Alfred Hamlet towards Op-die-berg (~44km surfaced).
- From Op-die-berg: DR1487 (Swartrug road) towards Ceres Karoo (~17km surfaced).
- DR1487 to DR2244 (Katbakkies road) towards Ceres Karoo (~36km gravel).
- MR316 (R355) continue ~25km towards DR2250.

## Saving your tyres
- Check tyres/pressures before leaving.
- Keep tyres inflated per manufacturer guidelines.
- Don't overload vehicle.
- Avoid the stony 'middle-mannetjie' and don't hug the road's left edge.
- Take two spares plus a tyre repair kit, snotplugs and a compressor/Tyre Weld.
- Keep spares accessible.
- No AfrikaBurn crew patrol/assistance outside the event on the R355.
- Tyre repair station at Tankwa Tented Camp; Tankwa Padstal folk may help too.
- Respect donkey carts/animals.
- Slow down when passing to reduce dust/rock kickup.

## DO NOT OVERTAKE WHEN THE ROAD AHEAD IS OBSCURED BY DUST
Cause of [fatal head-on collisions in the past](https://www.afrikaburn.org/binnekringblog/dont-drive-like-a-dick/).

## Directions to Quaggafontein

### From Cape Town
Take N1 North past Paarl, Worcester, De Doorns. 10km before Touwsrivier (160km from Cape Town), turn LEFT onto R46 towards Ceres (last fuel chance at Worcester/Touwsrivier). Continue ~40km to a T-junction, turn RIGHT onto R355 towards Calvinia/Sutherland. ~100m later hit dirt — slow down. Stay on R355 past Tankwa Padstal (last WiFi). ~22km past the Padstal, turn right (signposted Tankwa Karoo National Park) towards Quaggafontein. Continue ~33km; entrance on the right. Through the gate: Die Hek, Box Office (check-in, ticket scan), then Greeting Station.

### From Johannesburg
N1 South through Colesburg, Hanover, Richmond. From Richmond, 64km on N1 then right onto R63 to Victoria West, then follow signs to Calvinia. **Do not use the R354 (Middlepos) road** — 8 hours for ~180km. No phone signal from Richmond onward until back in this area (including at the event). Calvinia is the last place to get supplies (water, gas, petrol) — no shops after Calvinia. ~2km past Calvinia, turn left at 'Ceres R355' sign onto gravel R355; continue to the Tankwa Karoo National Park turnoff (~50km from Calvinia side), turn left, continue ~90km to entrance on the left. Do not exceed 70km/h.

- **Alternative Route 1**: continue on R355 ~140km past Stonehenge/Tankwa Tented Camp, watch for the Tankwa Karoo National Park signposted junction (turn left), then ~33km to entrance on the right.
- **Alternative Route 2**: N1 down to Touws River, then right up to R355, follow Cape Town directions (preferred route for Joburg creative crews).
- **Alternative Route 3**: N12 via Kimberley, Britstown, Victoria West to Three Sisters (rejoin N1), Touws River (fill up), R46 towards Ceres, R355 towards Calvinia via Tankwa Padstal, 22km after Padstal turn right towards national park, 33km further turn right into Quaggafontein.

### Via Bloem
N8 to Kimberley, N12 to Victoria West, right onto R63 (T-junction), then follow Johannesburg directions from Victoria West. Alternatively N1 to Touws River (Alt Route 2).

### From Durban
N3 to Harrismith, N5 via Bethlehem to Winberg, join N1 to Bloemfontein, then follow Bloemfontein directions.

### In a taxi
Taxi arrivals (with valid ticket) dropped at the Shuttle stop on 6ish between A & B Street; must walk to camp and arrange pickup there Sunday/Monday.
