Theme Camp Registration
V1 Scope Document | Preliminary | May 2026
Overview
Every theme camp must register with AfricaBurn. Registration captures what the camp is, what they'll provide,
how big they are, where they'd like to be placed, and what infrastructure/noise/safety considerations apply.
Currently this is a Google Form — unresponsive, hard to update after submission, and painful on both sides.
We have a complete copy of the actual Google Form (filled in for 2025/2026). The scope below is structured
around the real fields.
Registration Data — What Gets Captured
Based on the actual Google Form, grouped into logical sections.
Section 1: Camp Identity
Field Type Notes
Camp name Text
Contact email Email Camp lead's email
Camp description Long text 60-word limit enforced
Section 2: Leave No Trace (LNT)
Field Type Notes
LNT policy details Long text MOOP plan, grey water, waste streams
LNT Lead name Text Separate person from camp lead
LNT Lead phone Phone
LNT Lead email Email
Section 3: Participation & Gifting
Field Type Notes
Participation plan Long text Free-form — can be 500+ words
Operating hours Multi-select Morning / Day / Night / Late Night
Schedule detail Long text Optional extra detail
Gifting food? Yes/No

What's Wrong With the Google Form
(cid:127) No save and return — close the tab, lose everything
(cid:127) No updates after submission — can't amend if camp size changes or schedule finalises
(cid:127) No status visibility — submit and get silence until someone contacts you
(cid:127) No structure for AB reviewers — likely a spreadsheet of responses
(cid:127) Long free-text fields are hard to review — 500+ words of participation plans per camp
(cid:127) No connection to other workflows — registration data doesn't flow into container booking, water, etc.
(cid:127) No year-to-year continuity — returning camps start from scratch
(cid:127) Placement preferences are just text selections — no visual map

Key Design Opportunities (vs Google Form)
1. Save and return — draft state, resume where you left off
2. Section-by-section review — AB can approve parts and request changes on others
3. Year-to-year pre-fill — returning camps start with last year's data pre-loaded
4. Connected camp profile — registration data feeds into containers, water, ice, placement
5. Visual placement map — show the site map instead of text dropdowns
6. Status and comms — camps see where their registration is, get feedback, respond — all in one place
7. Dashboard & filtering — AB gets a proper management view instead of a spreadsheet
Confirmed Business Rules
(cid:127) Camp description: 60-word limit
(cid:127) Placement zones change year to year — must be configurable per edition
(cid:127) Camp codes assigned by AB at placement, not chosen by camp
(cid:127) Camp entity is persistent — yearly registrations hang off a permanent camp record
(cid:127) Camp lead handover supported via nomination or one-time invite link
(cid:127) Registration and ticketing are completely separate
(cid:127) Only lead, alt contact, and LNT lead tracked — population is just a number
(cid:127) LNT lead: contact details only, no app role
(cid:127) WAPs: separate allocation process, registration just captures the request
(cid:127) Registration is upstream of all other workflows — must be completed first

Relationship to Other Workflows
Registration is upstream of everything. It creates the camp profile that other workflows depend on:
(cid:127) Container Transport needs: camp profile, ERF, contacts
(cid:127) Water Delivery needs: camp profile, population
(cid:127) Ice Delivery needs: camp profile, population
(cid:127) Gas Delivery needs: camp profile
(cid:127) Placement needs: preferences, sound level, area, neighbour requests
Important open question: Artworks and possibly mutant vehicles may also access these downstream services.
If so, the upstream entity is not strictly 'theme camp' but a broader 'registered project.' Needs AB clarification.
